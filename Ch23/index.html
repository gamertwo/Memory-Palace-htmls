Why This Chapter Feels Different

Chapter 22 taught us how to build fields from tiny charge pieces. Chapter 23 pivots to the inverse mindset: choose a surface that matches the symmetry, then let flux expose the enclosed charge.

Suggested Continuity

  • Review the ring and disk pages in Chapter 22 if you want a quick symmetry refresher.
  • Start with Gauss's law, then flux, then the cylinder problem, the cube flux problem, convert flux into enclosed charge, use the direct enclosed-charge-to-flux example, move into symmetry-based field derivations, then do planar superposition and spherical symmetry.
