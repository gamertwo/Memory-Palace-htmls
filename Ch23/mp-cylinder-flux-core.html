Closed Cylinder in Uniform Field

This example is worth memorizing because it teaches cancellation clearly. The cylinder has three parts in the story: left cap, curved wall, right cap.

Start Formula
$$\Phi=\int_a \vec E\cdot d\vec A+\int_b \vec E\cdot d\vec A+\int_c \vec E\cdot d\vec A$$
End Formula
$$-EA+0+EA=0$$
Rooms

Room 1: Split the Surface

  • Break the cylinder into left cap, curved wall, and right cap.
  • That makes the flux easy to organize.

Room 2: Left Cap

  • The field points opposite the outward area vector there.
  • That contribution is negative.

Room 3: Curved Wall

  • The field runs parallel to the wall.
  • So the wall contribution is zero.

Room 4: Right Cap

  • The field and outward area vector point the same way.
  • That contribution is positive.

Room 5: Cancellation

  • Negative and positive cap flux cancel.
  • The total flux is zero.
Simple Summary

Left cap gives negative flux, the curved wall gives zero, and the right cap gives positive flux, so the total is zero.
