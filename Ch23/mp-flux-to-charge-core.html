Flux to Enclosed Charge

This is the reverse move. Instead of using charge to find flux, you start with the net flux and use Gauss's law to recover the enclosed charge.

Start Formula
$$\Phi \text{ is known}$$
End Formula
$$q_{enc}=\varepsilon_0\Phi$$
Rooms

Room 1: Start with Net Flux

  • The total flux has already been found.
  • This means the surface work is finished.

Room 2: Call Gauss's Law

  • Bring in Gauss's law as the conversion rule.
  • Flux and enclosed charge are tied together.

Room 3: Rearrange

  • Multiply both sides by epsilon naught.
  • Now the enclosed charge stands alone.

Room 4: Read the Sign

  • Positive flux means net positive enclosed charge.
  • Negative flux means net negative enclosed charge.
Simple Summary

When the net flux is already known, Gauss's law becomes a direct conversion: multiply by epsilon naught to get the net enclosed charge.
