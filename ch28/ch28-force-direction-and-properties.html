28-3 Force Behavior

Right-Hand Rule and Magnetic Force Properties

This page turns the formula into motion logic. First you find the direction of $$\vec v\times\vec B,$$ then you use the sign of charge to keep or reverse that direction, and finally you lock the zero-force, maximum-force, and perpendicular-force facts that make problems faster.

$$\vec F_B=q\vec v\times\vec B,\qquad F_B=|q|vB\sin\phi,\qquad \vec F_B\perp\vec v\text{ and }\vec F_B\perp\vec B$$
Chapter GuideOpen in Memory Builder
Quick Data
Positive Charge
Direction is the same as $$\vec v\times\vec B$$
Negative Charge
Direction is opposite to $$\vec v\times\vec B$$
Always Perpendicular
The magnetic force has no component along $$\vec v$$
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Use the Right-Hand Rule on $\vec v\times\vec B$

  • Point your fingers along $$\vec v$$ and curl them toward $$\vec B$$ through the smaller angle.
  • Your thumb then gives the direction of $$\vec v\times\vec B.$$
  • That is the force direction only for a positive charge.

Room 2: Correct the Direction for the Sign of Charge

  • If $$q>0,$$ then $$\vec F_B$$ points in the same direction as $$\vec v\times\vec B.$$
  • If $$q<0,$$ then multiplying by the negative charge reverses the direction.
  • That one sign check is often the difference between the right and wrong answer in diagrams.

Room 3: Derive the Zero and Maximum Cases from Sine

  • From $$F_B=|q|vB\sin\phi,$$ the force vanishes when $$\sin\phi=0.$$
  • So $$F_B=0$$ when $$\phi=0^\circ$$ or $$180^\circ,$$ meaning $$\vec v$$ is parallel or antiparallel to $$\vec B.$$
  • The force is largest when $$\sin\phi=1,$$ so the maximum occurs at $$\phi=90^\circ.$$

Room 4: Lock the Perpendicular Property

  • A cross product is perpendicular to both input vectors, so $$\vec F_B$$ is perpendicular to $$\vec v$$ and to $$\vec B.$$
  • That means the magnetic force does not push along the direction of motion.
  • The main effect is to bend the path rather than directly speed the particle up or slow it down.

Room 5: Convert the Unit Formula into Tesla

  • From the perpendicular form, $$B=F_B/(|q|v).$$
  • Using $$1\,\mathrm{C}=1\,\mathrm{A\cdot s},$$ the unit becomes $$1\,\mathrm{T}=\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{N}}{(1\,\mathrm{C})(1\,\mathrm{m/s})}=\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{N}}{1\,\mathrm{A\cdot m}}.$$
  • So the SI unit of magnetic field is the tesla.

Key Shortcut

Do the problem in this order: direction of $$\vec v\times\vec B$$, sign of charge, then the sine-based magnitude check.

Main Tidbit

The magnetic force is a steering force. That single picture explains why tracks curve in magnetic-field images.

Solution Flow

Find the cross-product direction first, then keep or flip it, then use the sine formula to read special cases.
