28-5 Hall Effect

Crossed Fields: The Hall Effect

This page keeps the Hall-effect derivation narrow and formula-first. Charges moving through a conductor in a magnetic field get pushed sideways, which builds an internal electric field. Equilibrium arrives when the electric and magnetic forces balance. That balance leads to the Hall voltage formula.

$$V=Ed,\qquad eE=ev_dB,\qquad v_d=\frac{J}{ne}=\frac{i}{neA},\qquad V=\frac{Bi}{nte}$$

Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Let the Magnetic Force Push the Carriers Sideways

  • Current carriers drifting through a strip in a magnetic field feel the sideways magnetic force $$\vec F_B=q\vec v_d\times\vec B.$$
  • That force drives the carriers toward one edge of the strip.
  • Charge separation begins immediately once the current and field are both present.

Room 2: Build the Internal Electric Field from Charge Separation

  • As carriers pile up on one side, the opposite side is left with excess opposite charge.
  • This separation creates an internal electric field across the width of the strip.
  • The corresponding potential difference is the Hall voltage.

Room 3: Write the Equilibrium Balance Equation

  • Equilibrium is reached when the electric force exactly cancels the magnetic force.
  • So $$eE=ev_dB.$$
  • Canceling the charge gives the clean field relation $$E=v_dB.$$

Room 4: Convert the Electric Field into Hall Voltage

  • Across a strip of width $$d,$$ the potential difference magnitude is $$V=Ed.$$
  • Substituting $$E=v_dB$$ gives $$V=v_dBd.$$
  • So the Hall voltage is directly tied to the drift speed.

Room 5: Replace Drift Speed with Current Density and Current

  • Use $$v_d=\dfrac{J}{ne}=\dfrac{i}{neA}. $$
  • For a strip with cross-sectional area $$A=td,$$ this becomes $$v_d=\dfrac{i}{netd}. $$
  • Substituting into $$V=v_dBd$$ gives $$V=\dfrac{Bi}{nte},$$ which matches the chapter result when the carrier density symbol is written as in the text.

Key Shortcut

Hall-effect derivation is a four-link chain: sideways magnetic push, charge separation, force balance, then substitute drift speed.

Main Tidbit

The Hall effect is valuable because the sign of the measured voltage can reveal whether the mobile carriers are negative or positive.

Solution Flow

Sideways push first, electric buildup second, force balance third, voltage formula last.
