28-4 Crossed Fields

Crossed Fields: Discovery of the Electron

This page keeps the Thomson idea in its most usable form. An electric field and a magnetic field act in opposite transverse directions on the moving particles. When the beam stops deflecting, the two forces balance, and that balance gives the particle speed directly. The main memory point is the cancellation equation.

$$|q|E=|q|vB,\qquad v=\frac{E}{B},\qquad \frac{|q|}{m}=\frac{yB^2L}{2E^2d}$$

Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Build the Thomson Beam Setup

  • Electrons leave the cathode and pass through a region with both an electric field and a magnetic field.
  • The two fields are arranged so each produces a sideways force on the beam.
  • When those sideways forces oppose one another, the beam can be made to travel straight.

Room 2: Write the Electric Deflection Idea First

  • Without the magnetic field, the electric field between the plates deflects the charged beam.
  • The earlier plate-deflection result is $$y=\dfrac{|q|EL^2}{2mvd}. $$
  • That equation becomes one ingredient in measuring the charge-to-mass ratio.

Room 3: Balance the Electric and Magnetic Forces

  • When the beam is undeflected, the sideways electric force and magnetic force must have equal magnitudes.
  • So $$|q|E=|q|vB.$$
  • The charge magnitude cancels immediately, leaving the speed relation $$v=E/B.$$

Room 4: Substitute the Speed into the Deflection Equation

  • Now replace $$v$$ in the deflection formula with $$E/B.$$
  • After substitution and rearrangement, the measured quantities give $$\dfrac{|q|}{m}=\dfrac{yB^2L}{2E^2d}. $$
  • This is the core derivation result of the crossed-fields experiment.

Room 5: Keep the Historical Takeaway Small and Useful

  • Thomson found that the particles in the beam were negatively charged and had a much larger charge-to-mass ratio than ions like hydrogen.
  • The physics memory point is not the biography but the method: use crossed fields to measure speed, then infer $$|q|/m.$$
  • That experimental logic is the part worth locking into long-term memory.

Key Shortcut

For crossed fields, first think cancellation, then speed, then substitution into the older deflection formula.

Main Tidbit

The beautiful simplification is that the charge cancels in $$|q|E=|q|vB,$$ so speed comes out before charge-to-mass ratio does.

Solution Flow

Set up the two forces, cancel them at zero deflection, solve for speed, then use that speed inside the beam-deflection relation.
