28-6 Circular Motion

A Circulating Charged Particle

This page keeps the circular-motion section formula-first. When a charged particle enters a uniform magnetic field with velocity perpendicular to the field, the magnetic force is always perpendicular to the motion and acts as the centripetal force. That gives radius, period, frequency, and angular frequency in one short derivation chain.

$$|q|vB=\frac{mv^2}{r},\qquad r=\frac{mv}{|q|B},\qquad T=\frac{2\pi m}{|q|B},\qquad f=\frac{|q|B}{2\pi m},\qquad \omega=\frac{|q|B}{m}$$

Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Identify the Magnetic Force as the Centripetal Force

  • For motion with $$\vec v\perp\vec B,$$ the magnetic force magnitude is $$F_B=|q|vB.$$
  • That force always points perpendicular to the velocity, so it bends the path instead of changing the speed.
  • In uniform circular motion, the needed inward force is $$F=mv^2/r.$$

Room 2: Set Magnetic Force Equal to Centripetal Force

  • Set $$|q|vB=mv^2/r.$$
  • This is the whole derivation hinge of the section.
  • Once this balance is written, every motion formula follows from algebra.

Room 3: Solve for the Radius

  • Cancel one factor of $$v$$ from $$|q|vB=mv^2/r.$$
  • Then solve for radius: $$r=\dfrac{mv}{|q|B}. $$
  • So faster particles make larger circles, and stronger fields make smaller circles.

Room 4: Derive the Period and Frequency

  • Use $$T=\dfrac{2\pi r}{v}. $$
  • Substitute $$r=mv/(|q|B)$$ to get $$T=\dfrac{2\pi m}{|q|B}. $$
  • Then $$f=1/T=\dfrac{|q|B}{2\pi m}. $$

Room 5: Finish with Angular Frequency and the Key Insight

  • Use $$\omega=2\pi f$$ to get $$\omega=\dfrac{|q|B}{m}. $$
  • The radius depends on speed, but $$T,$$ $$f,$$ and $$\omega$$ do not.
  • So particles with the same charge-to-mass ratio cycle with the same timing in the same field.

Key Shortcut

Write the force balance first. Once $$|q|vB=mv^2/r$$ is on the page, everything else is algebra.

Main Tidbit

The magnetic field controls the timing through $$|q|/m$$ and $$B,$$ while the speed only stretches the circle size.

Solution Flow

Magnetic force becomes centripetal force, then radius comes out, then all timing quantities follow.
