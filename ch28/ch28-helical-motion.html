28-6 Helical Paths

Helical Motion of a Charged Particle in a Magnetic Field

This page keeps the helical-path idea compact. Split the velocity into perpendicular and parallel components relative to the magnetic field. The perpendicular part drives circular motion, while the parallel part carries the particle forward along the field. The pitch is just forward speed times one revolution period.

$$v_\perp=v\sin\phi,\qquad v_\parallel=v\cos\phi,\qquad p=v_\parallel T=\frac{2\pi m(v\cos\phi)}{|q|B}$$

Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Split the Velocity into Parallel and Perpendicular Parts

  • Resolve the velocity into $$v_\perp=v\sin\phi$$ and $$v_\parallel=v\cos\phi.$$
  • The angle $$\phi$$ is the angle between the particle velocity and the field.
  • This split is the entire conceptual key to helical motion.

Room 2: Let the Perpendicular Component Make the Circle

  • The magnetic force acts only on the motion component perpendicular to the field.
  • So the circular part of the motion uses the same formulas as Section 28-6, but with $$v_\perp$$ in place of $$v.$$
  • That means the radius depends on the perpendicular component.

Room 3: Let the Parallel Component Carry the Particle Forward

  • The motion component parallel to $$\vec B$$ is not bent by the magnetic force.
  • So the particle keeps moving forward along the field direction while circling.
  • This forward drift is what turns the path into a helix.

Room 4: Derive the Pitch of the Helix

  • Pitch means forward distance traveled in one period of revolution.
  • So $$p=v_\parallel T. $$
  • Using $$v_\parallel=v\cos\phi$$ and $$T=2\pi m/(|q|B),$$ we get $$p=\dfrac{2\pi m(v\cos\phi)}{|q|B}. $$

Room 5: Keep the Magnetic Bottle Tidbit Small

  • If the field becomes stronger near the ends of a region, the particle can reflect before escaping.
  • When it bounces back and forth while spiraling, the particle is said to be trapped in a magnetic bottle.
  • Keep this as a concept tidbit, not the main memorization target.

Key Shortcut

For helical motion, split velocity first. Everything else comes from deciding which part bends and which part carries forward.

Main Tidbit

Pitch is not a new force formula. It is just distance equals speed times time, using the parallel speed and one revolution period.

Solution Flow

Split the velocity, assign jobs to each component, then multiply forward speed by one period.
