University Physics Chapters 23-24

Electric Flux, Gauss's Law, and Electric Potential

This combined mini-site is organized around two linked question families. Chapter 23 asks how symmetry turns electric-flux and Gauss's-law problems into fast field calculations. Chapter 24 asks how we trade force language for energy and potential language so hard electric-field motion problems become manageable. The pages are split into derivations, strategy pages, and sample problems with explicit reasons after each major step.
