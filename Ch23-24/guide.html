Do and Do Not

Do

  • Pick a Gaussian surface that matches the source symmetry, not one that merely encloses the charge.
  • Use outward area vectors for flux and track the sign of inward versus outward piercing.
  • Check whether the field is constant on the chosen surface before pulling it outside an integral.
  • In potential problems, remember potential is scalar, so contributions add algebraically.
  • Use work, potential difference, and energy as the same story told with different emphasis.

Do Not

  • Do not use Gauss's law blindly when the symmetry is wrong.
  • Do not confuse zero net flux with zero electric field everywhere.
  • Do not add electric-field magnitudes when the real quantity being asked is potential.
  • Do not forget equipotential surfaces are perpendicular to field lines.
  • Do not mix the sign of charge with the sign of potential difference carelessly.
