How To Study Chapter 29

The goal in Chapter 29 is to separate the chapter into two question types: field-from-current problems handled by Biot-Savart or Ampere's law, and device problems handled by the standard solenoid, toroid, and dipole formulas.

$$\text{Choose source rule} \rightarrow \text{use symmetry if possible} \rightarrow \text{set direction with RHR} \rightarrow \text{finish the sample cleanly}$$
Core Route

1. Name the Source

Ask what current distribution creates the field: wire, arc, cylinder, solenoid, toroid, or loop.

2. Pick the Law

Use Biot-Savart for direct geometry and Ampere's law for symmetry-heavy setups.

3. Lock Direction

Use the right-hand rule before you trust any magnitude result.

4. Use the Standard Formula

Once the model is recognized, Chapter 29 often collapses to a known result.

5. Read the Sample

Most sample problems are really about choosing the correct model and discarding unnecessary pieces.
