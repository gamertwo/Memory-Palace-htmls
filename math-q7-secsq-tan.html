Q7 · SecSq-Tan Coupled Form

Given: $\sec^2 x\tan y\,dx + \sec^2 y\tan x\,dy=0$

$$\sec^2 x\tan y\,dx + \sec^2 y\tan x\,dy=0$$
Step 1: Divide all terms by $\tan x\tan y$:
$$\frac{\sec^2 x\tan y}{\tan x\tan y}dx + \frac{\sec^2 y\tan x}{\tan x\tan y}dy=0$$
$$\frac{\sec^2 x}{\tan x}dx + \frac{\sec^2 y}{\tan y}dy=0$$
Step 2: Use derivative identity:
$$\frac{d}{du}\!\left(\ln|\tan u|\right)=\frac{\sec^2 u}{\tan u}$$
Step 3: Integrate each term:
$$\int\frac{\sec^2 x}{\tan x}dx+\int\frac{\sec^2 y}{\tan y}dy=\int 0$$
$$\ln|\tan x|+\ln|\tan y|=C$$
Step 4: Merge logs:
$$\ln|\tan x\tan y|=C\Rightarrow \tan x\tan y=C$$
$$\boxed{\tan x\tan y=C}$$
Quick verification: Differentiate $\tan x\tan y=C$ to recover $\sec^2 x\tan y\,dx+\tan x\sec^2 y\,dy=0$.
Memory cues:
Room 1
“Tan cancel room”: divide first by $\tan x\tan y$.
Room 2
$\sec^2/\tan$ is log-tan derivative fingerprint.
Room 3
Two logs merge into product: $\tan x\tan y=C$.
