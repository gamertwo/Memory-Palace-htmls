Solve the Following Differential Equation

Direct solution only.

$$\left(e^y+2\right)\sin x\,dx - e^y\cos x\,dy = 0$$
Rooms

Room 1: Start

  • Write the equation as given:
  • $$\left(e^y+2\right)\sin x\,dx - e^y\cos x\,dy = 0.$$

Room 2: Choose the Product

  • Take the candidate product
  • $$-\left(e^y+2\right)\cos x.$$

Room 3: Differentiate

  • Differentiate the candidate:
  • $$d\!\left[-\left(e^y+2\right)\cos x\right]=\left(e^y+2\right)\sin x\,dx-e^y\cos x\,dy.$$

Room 4: Replace the Left Side

  • So the equation becomes
  • $$d\!\left[-\left(e^y+2\right)\cos x\right]=0.$$

Room 5: Integrate

  • Integrate both sides:
  • $$-\left(e^y+2\right)\cos x=C.$$

Room 6: Clean the Sign

  • Absorb the minus sign into the constant:
  • $$\left(e^y+2\right)\cos x=C.$$

Room 7: Final Answer

  • $$\boxed{\left(e^y+2\right)\cos x=C}$$
Memory Clues

Clue 1

$\sin x\,dx$ matches $-\cos x$.

Clue 2

$e^y\,dy$ matches the derivative of $e^y+2$.

Clue 3

Choose the sign that makes the differential match exactly.

Clue 4

Absorb any overall minus sign into $C$.
