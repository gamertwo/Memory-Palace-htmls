Example 9-1

The Flat Step Ground Becomes One White Pillar

This first Chapter 9 scene stays simple on purpose. You stand on the unit-step ground, sweep across time with the curved blade, and watch the exponential orb die at infinity until only a white pillar over the spiral vortex remains.

$$f(t)=u(t), \qquad F(s)=\mathcal{L}\{u(t)\}=\frac{1}{s}$$
Shape Guide Open In Builder
Quick Result
Given
$$f(t)=u(t)$$
Transform
$$F(s)=\dfrac{1}{s}$$
Main image
Flat white ground, then a white pillar standing over the dark $$s$$-vortex.
Linear Review: Actual Concept First
Linear Room 1

Start From The Definition

Use the Laplace definition directly:

$$F(s)=\int_0^{\infty} f(t)e^{-st}\,dt$$

Now substitute $$f(t)=u(t).$$

Because $$u(t)=1$$ for $$t\ge 0,$$ the integral becomes

$$F(s)=\int_0^{\infty} e^{-st}\,dt$$

Linear Room 2

Integrate The Exponential Carefully

The antiderivative is

$$\int e^{-st}\,dt=-\frac{e^{-st}}{s}$$

So

$$F(s)=\left[-\frac{e^{-st}}{s}\right]_0^{\infty}$$

Linear Room 3

Evaluate The Upper Limit First

At the upper limit, $$e^{-st}\to 0$$ as $$t\to\infty$$ provided $$\Re\{s\}>0.$$

That means the top contribution is

$$-\frac{0}{s}=0$$

Linear Room 4

Use The Lower Limit At $$t=0$$

At $$t=0,$$ the exponential becomes

$$e^0=1$$

So the lower-limit term is

$$-\left(-\frac{1}{s}\right)=\frac{1}{s}$$

Therefore,

$$F(s)=\frac{1}{s}$$

Concept Summary

The unit step is the first anchor transform of the chapter. Once $$u(t)$$ becomes 1 over the integration interval, the whole problem reduces to the exponential limit story that ends at $$1/s.$$

Rooms: Wall Maria Outer Gate

Room 1: The Flat Ground Turns The Signal On

You stand on a completely flat white training ground. That ground is $$u(t),$$ because after the signal turns on, it stays unchanged across the whole road of time.

  • Flat ground means constant value 1.
  • The road starts at $$t=0.$$
  • The same ground continues forward toward infinity.

Room 2: The Curved Blade Sweeps Across The Road

A glowing exponential orb appears over the time road while a dark spiral vortex pulls on it. Then the curved ODM blade cuts through the whole route. That slash is the integral and the moving orb is $$e^{-st}.$$

  • Curved blade means integration.
  • Orb plus vortex means $$e^{-st}.$$
  • The cut turns the time process into one stacked fraction shape.

Room 3: The Infinity Gate Kills The Orb

You run to the outer gate at the horizon and the glowing orb fades to nothing there. That stores the upper-limit fact that the exponential contribution vanishes at infinity.

  • Infinity gate means the upper limit.
  • The orb dies there, so the upper term becomes zero.
  • The condition is that the decay is strong enough: $$\Re\{s\}>0.$$

Room 4: A White Pillar Freezes Above The Vortex

Back at $$t=0,$$ the orb is fully intact and snaps into a bright white pillar equal to 1. The final frozen picture is that pillar standing over the spiral vortex, which is exactly $$1/s.$$

  • At zero, the orb becomes 1.
  • The bottom object stays the $$s$$-vortex.
  • The whole page should collapse to the image $$1/s.$$
Memory Hook

Flat step ground, integrate, infinity kills the orb, pillar remains over vortex.
