Linear Review: Actual Concept First
Linear Room 1

Transform The Cosine And Sine Terms With The Exponential Shift

Start from

$$f(t)=\left(e^{-10t}\cos200t+0.05e^{-10t}\sin200t\right)u(t)$$

Use the shifted cosine and sine Laplace forms:

$$\mathcal{L}\{e^{-at}\cos\omega t\,u(t)\}=\frac{s+a}{(s+a)^2+\omega^2}$$

$$\mathcal{L}\{e^{-at}\sin\omega t\,u(t)\}=\frac{\omega}{(s+a)^2+\omega^2}$$

Here $$a=10$$ and $$\omega=200,$$ so

$$\mathcal{L}\{e^{-10t}\cos200t\,u(t)\}=\frac{s+10}{(s+10)^2+200^2}$$

$$\mathcal{L}\{0.05e^{-10t}\sin200t\,u(t)\}=0.05\cdot\frac{200}{(s+10)^2+200^2}=\frac{10}{(s+10)^2+200^2}$$

Linear Room 2

Combine The Terms Into One Fraction

Both transformed terms already have the same denominator, so combine only the numerators:

$$F(s)=\frac{s+10}{(s+10)^2+200^2}+\frac{10}{(s+10)^2+200^2}$$

That gives

$$F(s)=\frac{s+20}{(s+10)^2+200^2}$$

This is the clean rational form you need before plotting anything.

Linear Room 3

Find The Zero From The Numerator

The numerator is $$s+20.$$ Set it equal to zero:

$$s+20=0$$

So the zero is

$$s=-20$$

That is why the circle lies on the negative real axis.

Linear Room 4

Find The Pole Pair From The Shifted Quadratic

The denominator is

$$ (s+10)^2+200^2=0 $$

Move the constant term:

$$ (s+10)^2=-200^2 $$

Take the square root:

$$ s+10=\pm j200 $$

Then solve for $$s$$:

$$ s=-10\pm j200 $$

So the final plot has one zero at $$-20$$ and two poles at $$-10+j200$$ and $$-10-j200.$$

Concept Summary

This page is the clean shifted-pair example. The left shift comes from the exponential, the pair comes from the quadratic, and the numerator gives the single real zero at $$-20.$$
