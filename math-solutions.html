Q1. $\frac{dx}{x}=\tan y\,dy$

$$\int\frac{dx}{x}=\int\tan y\,dy\Rightarrow \ln|x|=-\ln|\cos y|+C\Rightarrow x\cos y=C$$
Final: $\boxed{x\cos y=C}$

Q2. $(2xy+x^2)\dfrac{dy}{dx}=3y^2+2xy$

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{3y^2+2xy}{2xy+x^2},\;y=vx,\;\frac{dy}{dx}=v+x\frac{dv}{dx}$$ $$v+x\frac{dv}{dx}=\frac{3v^2+2v}{2v+1}\Rightarrow \frac{2v+1}{v^2+v}dv=\frac{dx}{x}$$ $$\ln(v^2+v)=\ln x+\ln C\Rightarrow v^2+v=Cx\Rightarrow y^2+xy=Cx^3$$
Final: $\boxed{y^2+xy=Cx^3}$

Q3. $\frac{dx}{x}=\cot y\,dy$

$$\ln|x|=\ln|\sin y|+C\Rightarrow \frac{x}{\sin y}=C$$
Final: $\boxed{\frac{x}{\sin y}=C}$

Q4. $\frac{dx}{x}=\sec y\tan y\,dy$

$$\ln|x|=\sec y + C\Rightarrow x=Ce^{\sec y}$$
Final: $\boxed{x=Ce^{\sec y}}$
