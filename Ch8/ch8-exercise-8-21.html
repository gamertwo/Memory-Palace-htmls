Exercise 8-21

Replace the Capacitor with an Inductor and Read the New Filter

This page is the high-pass mirror of the RC low-pass movie. You write the output-to-input ratio for the inductor voltage, take its magnitude, inspect the low- and high-frequency limits, and then read the cutoff rule. The memory emphasis is contrast: the new filter blocks low frequencies and passes high frequencies.

$$left|\frac{V_L}{V_S}\right|=\frac{\omega}{\sqrt{\omega^2+(R/L)^2}},\qquad \omega_c=R/L$$

What You Are Asked To Find
Given
Replace the capacitor in Figure 8-29 with an inductor.
Find
Find the ratio $V_L/V_S$ and comment on how it behaves as frequency changes.
Target
You are being asked to identify the filter type and its cutoff rule as well.
Quick Data
Transfer Ratio
$$\frac{V_L}{V_S}=\frac{j\omega}{j\omega+R/L}$$
Magnitude
$$\left|\frac{V_L}{V_S}\right|=\frac{\omega}{\sqrt{\omega^2+(R/L)^2}}$$
Cutoff
$$\omega_c=R/L$$
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Write the Output-to-Input Ratio for the Inductor

  • With the capacitor replaced by an inductor, the output is now taken across the inductor.
  • Using the voltage-divider idea gives $$\frac{V_L}{V_S}=\frac{j\omega L}{R+j\omega L}. $$
  • Dividing numerator and denominator by $$L$$ gives the compact form $$\frac{V_L}{V_S}=\frac{j\omega}{j\omega+R/L}. $$

Rooam 2: Take the Magnitude of the Ratio

  • Now take the magnitude to study the filter behavior.
  • That gives $$\left|\frac{V_L}{V_S}\right|=\frac{\omega}{\sqrt{\omega^2+(R/L)^2}}.$$
  • This is the high-pass partner of the low-pass magnitude formula.

Room 3: Check the Low- and High-Frequency Limits

  • As $$\omega o0,$$ the numerator goes to zero, so the output magnitude goes to zero.
  • As $$\omega o\infty,$$ the numerator and denominator are dominated by $$\omega,$$ so the magnitude approaches $$1.$$
  • So low frequencies are blocked and high frequencies pass.

Room 4: Read the Filter Type and the Cutoff Rule

  • Because high frequencies pass more strongly, this is a high-pass filter.
  • Its cutoff occurs when the output magnitude reaches $$1/\sqrt2$$ of the input magnitude.
  • For this RL high-pass form, the cutoff frequency is $$\omega_c=R/L.$$

Room 5: Lock the Contrast with the RC Low-Pass Page

  • The low-pass RC page had cutoff $$\omega_c=1/RC.$$
  • This high-pass RL page has cutoff $$\omega_c=R/L.$$
  • Memorize them as a contrast pair: RC low-pass drops with frequency, RL high-pass rises with frequency.

Key Shortcut

Memorize the contrast pair: RC low-pass uses $$1/(1+j\omega RC),$$ while RL high-pass uses the inductor-divider ratio and rises with frequency.

Why This Flow Matters

This page closes the chapter by turning one filter movie into its mirror image. That contrast makes both pages easier to remember.
