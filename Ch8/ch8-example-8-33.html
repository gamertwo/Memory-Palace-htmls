Example 8-33

Use the Mesh-Current Method to Find $$V_2$$ and $$Z_{IN}$$

This circuit contains a dependent source, so the important bookkeeping step is to keep the control voltage $$V_X=400(I_A-I_B)$$ tied to the mesh currents at all times.

$$V_2=10.2\angle-8.00^\circ\text{ V},\qquad Z_{IN}=450+j463~\Omega$$
Major GuideOpen in Memory Builder
What You Are Asked To Find
Unknowns
The output voltage $$V_2$$ and the input impedance $$Z_{IN}.$$
Method
Choose clockwise mesh currents $$I_A$$ and $$I_B,$$ write two KVL equations, then eliminate the control variable $$V_X.$$
Control Relation
$$V_X=400(I_A-I_B).$$
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Define the Mesh Currents and the Control Voltage

  • Let $$I_A$$ be the clockwise current in the left mesh and $$I_B$$ the clockwise current in the right mesh.
  • The 400-$$\Omega$$ resistor is shared by both meshes, so $$V_X=400(I_A-I_B).$$

Room 2: Write KVL Around Mesh $$A$$

  • Source rise: $$-10\angle0^\circ$$
  • Series drop: $$(200+j250)I_A$$
  • Shared-resistor drop: $$400(I_A-I_B)$$
  • KVL gives $$-10\angle0^\circ+(200+j250)I_A+400(I_A-I_B)=0$$ which simplifies to $$(600+j250)I_A-400I_B=10\angle0^\circ.$$

Room 3: Write KVL Around Mesh $$B$$

  • The second loop gives $$400(I_B-I_A)+(50-j500)I_B+2V_X=0.$$
  • In standard form: $$-400I_A+(450-j500)I_B=-2V_X.$$

Room 4: Eliminate $$V_X$$

  • Substitute $$V_X=400(I_A-I_B)$$ into the second equation.
  • Then $$-2V_X=-800(I_A-I_B).$$
  • This reduces mesh $$B$$ to $$400I_A+(-350-j500)I_B=0.$$

Room 5: Solve for the Mesh Currents

  • The system is $$ (600+j250)I_A-400I_B=10\angle0^\circ $$ and $$ 400I_A+(-350-j500)I_B=0. $$
  • Solving gives $$I_A=10.8-j11.1\text{ mA},\qquad I_B=-1.93-j9.95\text{ mA}. $$

Room 6: Compute $$V_2$$ and $$Z_{IN}$$

  • $$V_2=2V_X+50I_B=800(I_A-I_B)+50I_B=800I_A-750I_B$$
  • Substituting the currents gives $$V_2=10.1-j1.42\text{ V}=10.2\angle-8.00^\circ\text{ V}. $$
  • Since the source current is $$I_A,$$ $$Z_{IN}=\frac{10\angle0^\circ}{I_A}=450+j463~\Omega.$$
