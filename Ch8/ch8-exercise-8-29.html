Exercise 8-29

Find the Thevenin Circuit Seen by the Inductor and Then Find $$I_x$$

This exercise repeats the Thevenin movie on a different topology. You remove the inductor, find the terminal Thevenin pair seen by that branch, and then restore the inductor as the load to get the current through it.

$$V_T=0.08925+j1.3839\text{ V},\quad Z_T=230.8-j153.8~\Omega,\quad I_x=5.852\angle99.44^\circ\text{ mA}$$
Major GuideOpen in Memory Builder
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Remove the Inductor and Look into Its Terminals

  • The branch of interest is the inductor branch.
  • To find the Thevenin equivalent seen by it, remove the inductor first.
  • This exposes the terminal voltage and look-back impedance of the rest of the circuit.

Why This Step

Thevenin is always taken from the load's point of view. If the inductor stays connected, it contaminates the very network you are trying to reduce.

Room 2: State the Thevenin Pair

  • The terminal Thevenin voltage is $$V_T=0.08925+j1.3839\text{ V}.$$
  • The look-back impedance is $$Z_T=230.8-j153.8~\Omega.$$
  • These two quantities replace the entire rest of the circuit.

Why This Step

This is the compression step. Once the fixed network is replaced by a Thevenin pair, the remaining current calculation is short and direct.

Room 3: Put the Inductor Back as the Load

  • Restore the inductor branch to the Thevenin terminals.
  • The current $$I_x$$ is now the load current of a reduced one-source circuit.
  • Use the Thevenin source and impedance together with the inductor impedance.

Why This Step

Thevenin does not solve the load current by itself; it makes the load current easy to solve after the network has been reduced.

Room 4: State the Final Inductor Current

  • The resulting current is $$I_x=5.852\angle99.44^\circ\text{ mA}.$$
  • This is the current through the reconnected inductor branch.
  • The whole route was: remove, reduce, restore, solve.

Why This Step

This final step is small only because the circuit was compressed first. Without Thevenin, the original branch network would be much less transparent.
