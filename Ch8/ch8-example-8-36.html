Example 8-36

Calculate Delivered Power and the Maximum Average Power Available at an Interface

Part (a) asks what a specific 200-$$\Omega$$ load receives. Part (b) asks what the interface could deliver at best if the load were adjustable. Those are related questions, but not the same question.

$$V_T=\sqrt{5}\angle-63.4^\circ\text{ V},\quad Z_T=40-j80~\Omega,\quad P_{200\Omega}=7.81\text{ mW},\quad P_{MAX}=15.6\text{ mW}$$
Major GuideOpen in Memory Builder
What You Are Asked To Find
Circuit Data
$$v_s(t)=5\cos(10^6 t)\text{ V},\quad R=200\ \Omega,\quad C=0.01\,\mu\text{F},\quad R_L=200\ \Omega.$$
Part (a)
Find the average power delivered to the 200-$$\Omega$$ load.
Part (b)
Find the maximum average power available at the interface and the load that achieves it.
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Convert the Capacitor to an Impedance

  • $$\omega=10^6\text{ rad/s}$$ and $$C=0.01\,\mu\text{F}=10^{-8}\text{ F}.$$
  • So $$Z_C=\frac{1}{j\omega C}=\frac{1}{j(10^6)(10^{-8})}=-j100~\Omega. $$

Room 2: Find the Thevenin Voltage at the Interface

  • With the load removed, the interface voltage is the capacitor voltage of the divider:
  • $$V_T=\frac{Z_C}{R+Z_C}V_S=\frac{-j100}{200-j100}(5\angle0^\circ)=1-j2\text{ V}. $$
  • So $$V_T=\sqrt{5}\angle-63.4^\circ\text{ V}. $$

Room 3: Find the Thevenin Impedance

  • The short-circuit current is $$I_N=\frac{5\angle0^\circ}{200}=0.025+j0\text{ A}. $$
  • Then $$Z_T=\frac{V_T}{I_N}=\frac{1-j2}{0.025}=40-j80~\Omega. $$

Room 4: Part (a) Use the Specific Load $$R_L=200\ \Omega$$

  • The load current is $$I_L=\frac{V_T}{Z_T+R_L}=\frac{1-j2}{240-j80}=0.00625-j0.00625\text{ A}. $$
  • So $$|I_L|=8.84\text{ mA}$$ and $$P_{200\Omega}=\frac{1}{2}(200)(0.00884)^2=7.81\text{ mW}. $$

Room 5: Part (b) Use the Maximum-Power Condition

  • The conjugate-matched load is $$Z_{L,MAX}=Z_T^*=40+j80~\Omega. $$
  • The maximum average power is $$P_{MAX}=\frac{|V_T|^2}{8R_T}=\frac{5}{8(40)}=15.6\text{ mW}. $$

Room 6: Final Answer

  • $$V_T=\sqrt{5}\angle-63.4^\circ\text{ V},\qquad Z_T=40-j80~\Omega.$$
  • $$P_{200\Omega}=7.81\text{ mW},\qquad P_{MAX}=15.6\text{ mW},\qquad Z_{L,MAX}=40+j80~\Omega.$$
