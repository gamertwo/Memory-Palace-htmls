Application Example 8-11

Derive the Resonant Frequency of the RLC Network

This page is a derivation movie. You first reduce the parallel resistor-capacitor part into one impedance, rewrite it in rectangular form, add the inductor in series, isolate the imaginary part of the total impedance, and then force that imaginary part to zero to get the resonant frequency. The memory emphasis is on the reactance lane only: resonance happens when that lane vanishes.

$$\omega_0=\sqrt{\frac{1}{LC}-\frac{1}{(RC)^2}}$$

You are being asked to derive the resonance condition, not plug in numbers yet.
Quick Data
Parallel Block
$$Z_{RC}=\frac{R}{1+j\omega RC}$$
Total Impedance
$$Z_{EQ}=\frac{R}{1+(\omega RC)^2}+j\left[\omega L-\frac{\omega R^2C}{1+(\omega RC)^2}\right]$$
Resonance Rule
Set $$X_{EQ}(\omega)=0$$ to solve for $$\omega_0$$
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Reduce the Parallel RC Part First

  • The resistor and capacitor form the inside branch, so reduce that block before touching the inductor.
  • The parallel combination is $$Z_{RC}=\frac{1}{\frac{1}{R}+j\omega C}=\frac{R}{1+j\omega RC}. $$
  • This is the first memory target because everything else in the derivation is built from this one block.

Room 2: Rewrite $$Z_{RC}$$ in Rectangular Form

  • Multiply by the complex conjugate of the denominator to separate real and imaginary parts.
  • $$Z_{RC}=\frac{R}{1+j\omega RC}\cdot\frac{1-j\omega RC}{1-j\omega RC}=\frac{R}{1+(\omega RC)^2}-j\frac{\omega R^2C}{1+(\omega RC)^2}. $$
  • Now the equivalent resistance and reactance of the branch are visible.

Room 3: Add the Inductor in Series

  • The inductor impedance is $$Z_L=j\omega L.$$
  • So the total input impedance becomes $$Z_{EQ}=Z_L+Z_{RC}. $$
  • That gives $$Z_{EQ}=\frac{R}{1+(\omega RC)^2}+j\left[\omega L-\frac{\omega R^2C}{1+(\omega RC)^2}\right].$$
  • The real part is always nonnegative; the resonance question lives entirely in the imaginary lane.

Room 4: Force the Reactance Lane to Zero

  • At resonance, the total reactance must vanish, so set $$X_{EQ}(\omega)=\omega L-\frac{\omega R^2C}{1+(\omega RC)^2}=0.$$
  • Solving that equation for $$\omega$$ gives $$\omega_0=\sqrt{\frac{1}{LC}-\frac{1}{(RC)^2}}.$$
  • This formula is the real memory prize of the page.

Room 5: Interpret What the Formula Means

  • When $$\omega>\omega_0,$$ the total reactance is positive and the circuit behaves inductively.
  • When $$\omega<\omega_0,$$ the total reactance is negative and the circuit behaves capacitively.
  • At $$\omega=\omega_0,$$ the input impedance is purely resistive even though the circuit still contains both $$L$$ and $$C$$.

Key Shortcut

Memorize the derivation as one chain: build $$Z_{RC}$$, rectangularize it, add $$j\omega L$$, isolate the imaginary lane, set it to zero.
