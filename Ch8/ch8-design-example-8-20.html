Design Example 8-20

Use the Thevenin Equivalent to Choose a Load

This page turns Thevenin into a design shortcut. First remove the load and find the Thevenin voltage and look-back impedance. Then put the load back as $$Z_L=R_L+jX_L$$ and force the divider equation to deliver the required output phasor.

$$V_T=35.35\angle-45^\circ\text{ V},\quad Z_T=500-j500~\Omega,\quad Z_L=235.3-j58.8~\Omega$$
Major GuideOpen in Memory Builder
What You Are Asked To Find
Given
Use the circuit of Fig. 8-42 in the phasor domain.
Find
Find the Thevenin equivalent seen by the load $$Z_L,$$ then choose $$Z_L$$ so that $$V_L=10\angle-90^\circ\text{ V}.$$
Design Pattern
Reduce the fixed network first, then design only the load.
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Remove Load and Find Thevenin Voltage

  • Remove $$Z_L$$ (open circuit)
  • Now no current flows into load
  • So output is just divider of fixed network

What is happening?

Open circuit means: $$I=0$$ So voltage is directly set by network.

Result

$$V_T=35.35\angle-45^\circ$$

Where did 35.35 come from?

From voltage division magnitude.

And: $$35.35 = \frac{50}{\sqrt{2}}$$ shows a 45° relationship.

Room 2: Find Thevenin Impedance

  • Turn OFF source
  • Voltage source → short
  • Look back into circuit

Result

$$Z_T=500-j500$$

Convert to Polar

$$|Z_T|=\sqrt{500^2+500^2}=707$$
$$\angle Z_T=\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{-500}{500}\right)=-45^\circ$$

Meaning

Circuit behaves like:
$$707\angle-45^\circ$$

Room 3: Set Up Design Equation

  • Let: $$Z_L=R_L+jX_L$$
  • Use divider: $$V_L=\frac{Z_L}{Z_L+Z_T}V_T$$

Substitute known values

$$10\angle-90^\circ = \frac{Z_L}{Z_L+(500-j500)}(35.35\angle-45^\circ)$$

Key idea

You are forcing the circuit to produce a desired output.

Room 4: Solve for Load

  • Multiply both sides
  • Convert to rectangular form
  • Separate real and imaginary parts

Why rectangular?

Because:
Real part → gives equation 1 Imag part → gives equation 2

Result

$$R_L=235.3$$ $$X_L=-58.8$$

Meaning of X_L

Negative → capacitive load

Room 5: Final Load

  • Combine results:

Final Answer

$$Z_L=235.3 - j58.8$$

Big Idea

Thevenin converts a complex circuit into:

ONE source + ONE impedance → then design becomes easy

Core Pattern

Reduce → Replace → Design
