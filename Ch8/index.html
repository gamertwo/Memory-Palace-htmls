Fast Review Rule

1. Name the Pattern

Decide whether the problem is a conversion, addition, impedance reduction, transfer-ratio, superposition, or equivalent-source problem.

2. Check Frequency

Before adding or transforming reactive circuits, make sure the operating frequency is understood and current.

3. Choose the Best Form

Use rectangular form for addition, polar form for multiplication and division, and admittance when the network is parallel.

4. Finish in the Right Domain

Return to the time domain only when the final phasor is complete and the chapter actually wants a waveform.
