Fast Review Rule

1. Catch

Catch the given signal or phasor and decide the job: to phasor, to sinusoid, add, or differentiate.

2. Match

Check frequency before adding anything. Phasor addition needs one common $$\omega.$$

3. Convert

Move between polar and rectangular in the simplest direction for the task.

4. Finish

Return to the time domain only after the phasor result is clean and boxed.
