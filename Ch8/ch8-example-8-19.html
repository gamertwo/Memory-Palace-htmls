Example 8-19

Transform a Thevenin Circuit into a Norton Circuit

This page starts the source-transformation movie for phasor circuits. The whole logic is simple: the Norton current is just the Thevenin voltage divided by the Thevenin impedance, and the Norton impedance is unchanged. The only real work is clean complex division.

$$I_N=\frac{V_T}{Z_T}=1.07\angle56.6^\circ\text{ A},\qquad Z_N=Z_T=100-j50~\Omega$$
Major GuideOpen in Memory Builder
What You Are Asked To Find
Given
The circuit in Fig. 8-41(a) has $$V_T=120\angle30^\circ\text{ V}$$ and $$Z_T=100-j50~\Omega.$$
Find
Find the Norton circuit of Fig. 8-41(b).
Key Rule
Use $$I_N=V_T/Z_T$$ and keep the same impedance.
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Start with the Source-Transformation Rule

  • A Thevenin source becomes a Norton source by Ohm's law.
  • The new current is $$I_N=V_T/Z_T.$$
  • The impedance does not change during the transformation.

Why This Step

This locks the rule before any arithmetic. Source transformation is not a new theorem every time; it is one reusable phasor move.

Room 2: Put the Impedance into Polar Form

  • Write $$Z_T=100-j50$$ in polar form.
  • Its magnitude-angle form is approximately $$112\angle-26.6^\circ~\Omega.$$
  • This makes the division much easier to read.

Why This Step

Phasor division is cleaner in polar form because magnitudes divide and angles subtract. This is a speed move, not just a style preference.

Room 3: Divide the Thevenin Voltage by the Impedance

  • $$I_N=\frac{120\angle30^\circ}{112\angle-26.6^\circ}.$$
  • That gives $$I_N=1.07\angle56.6^\circ\text{ A}.$$
  • This is the Norton source current.

Why This Step

This is the only actual computation on the page. Once the division is done, the transformed source is already known.

Room 4: Keep the Impedance Unchanged

  • The Norton impedance equals the Thevenin impedance.
  • So $$Z_N=Z_T=100-j50~\Omega.$$
  • The final Norton circuit is a current source in parallel with this same impedance.

Why This Step

This prevents a common mistake: only the source type changes. The terminal behavior must stay identical, so the impedance remains the same.
