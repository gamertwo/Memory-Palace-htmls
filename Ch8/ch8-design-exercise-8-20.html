Design Exercise 8-20

Choose R and C for a Low-Pass Filter with a Target Cutoff

This page is the design child of Application Example 8-14. You carry in the cutoff formula, rearrange it into a simple product rule, choose a practical resistor or capacitor, and then let that choice determine the other part. The memory emphasis is that many answers are possible, but every valid answer must satisfy the same product.

$$RC=1/\omega_c=0.001,\qquad ext{one choice is }R=1 ext{ k}\Omega, C=1\,\mu ext{F}$$
Major GuideOpen in Memory Builder
What You Are Asked To Find
Given
Use the low-pass filter of Figure 8-29 and the target cutoff $\omega_c=1000 ext{ rad/s}. $
Find
Choose standard values of $R$ and $C$ that realize that cutoff.
Target
This is a design question, so many answers are possible if they satisfy the same product rule.
Quick Data
Target Cutoff
$$\omega_c=1000 ext{ rad/s}$$
Design Rule
$$RC=1/\omega_c=0.001$$
One Simple Pair
$$R=1 ext{ k}\Omega,quad C=1\,\mu ext{F}$$
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Bring in the Low-Pass Cutoff Formula

  • From the low-pass filter page, $$\omega_c=1/RC.$$
  • This is the design formula you must hold before you pick any part values.

Room 2: Rearrange into a Product Rule

  • Solve the cutoff formula for the product $$RC.$$
  • That gives $$RC=\frac{1}{\omega_c}=\frac{1}{1000}=0.001.$$
  • Now the problem becomes a practical parts choice rather than a new derivation.

Room 3: Choose One Practical Component First

  • There are many valid solutions, so choose one convenient component value first.
  • A simple textbook-friendly choice is $$R=1 ext{ k}\Omega.$$
  • That leaves the capacitor to be determined by the fixed product.

Room 4: Let the Product Pull Out the Other Component

  • Use $$C=\frac{0.001}{R}=\frac{0.001}{1000}=10^{-6} ext{ F}. $$
  • So $$C=1\,\mu ext{F}. $$
  • This pair satisfies the target cutoff exactly.

Room 5: State the Design Logic Clearly

  • One acceptable design is $$R=1 ext{ k}\Omega$$ and $$C=1\,\mu ext{F}. $$
  • Other pairs are possible, but all valid answers must satisfy $$RC=0.001.$$
  • That is the main design memory: fixed cutoff means fixed product.

Key Shortcut

For a first-order low-pass design, memorize the sentence: target cutoff fixes the product $$RC$$.

Why This Flow Matters

This page turns the filter idea into a design reflex. Once $$\omega_c=1/RC$$ is memorized as a product rule, you can choose practical part values quickly instead of feeling stuck by the open-ended wording.
