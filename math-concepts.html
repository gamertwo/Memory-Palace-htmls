Patterns and Concepts in One Place

This is your single exam-readiness center: triggers, method rules, anti-mistake checks, and final-form patterns for both separable and homogeneous first-order differential equations.

Pattern A · Separable

Recognition Trigger

Equation can be put as $f(x)dx=g(y)dy$ directly.

$$\frac{dx}{x}=\tan y\,dy$$

Core Move

Integrate both sides and compress logs cleanly.

$$\int\frac{dx}{x}=\int\tan y\,dy$$

Answer Skeleton

After integration and constant absorption.

$$\ln|F(x,y)|=C\Rightarrow F(x,y)=C$$
Pattern B · Homogeneous

Recognition Trigger

Numerator and denominator same degree in $x,y$.

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{3y^2+2xy}{2xy+x^2}$$

Substitution Rule

Put $y=vx$ to reduce to separable in $v,x$.

$$y=vx,\quad \frac{dy}{dx}=v+x\frac{dv}{dx}$$

Back-substitute

After integrating in $v,x$, replace $v=\dfrac{y}{x}$.

$$\boxed{y^2+xy=Cx^3}$$
Exam Readiness Checklist
  • 1. Type test first: Separable or homogeneous?
  • 2. Sign discipline: $\int\tan y\,dy=-\ln|\cos y|$ (minus matters).
  • 3. Keep absolute values: in logarithms until final constant absorption.
  • 4. Verify quickly: Differentiate final answer and recover original DE.
  • 5. Homogeneous flow: substitute $y=vx$ then separate, integrate, back-substitute.
