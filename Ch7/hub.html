⬡ The One Skeleton
Every single problem below fits:   $x(t) = x(\infty) + [x(0) - x(\infty)]\,e^{-t/\tau}$
Your job: extract $\tau$, $x(0)$, $x(\infty)$ — then fill the skeleton.

🏛 The 5-Move Algorithm
I · Recognize
See $e^{-kt}$?
First-order circuit. Solution skeleton known.
II · Flip
$\tau = 1/k$
Exponent coefficient → flip it to get τ.
III · Freeze/Melt
$t=0$ & $t\to\infty$
$e^0=1$ → initial. $e^{-\infty}=0$ → final.
IV · Split
$B = x(0)-x(\infty)$
Natural response amplitude = gap to close.
V · Blueprint
$\tau\dot{x}+x=x(\infty)u(t)$
Fill skeleton. Verify at t=0 and t→∞.
