Design Exercise 7-35

Design a series RLC circuit with $$\zeta=1.5$$ and $$\omega_0=50\ \text{krad/s}.$$ The capacitor is fixed at $$0.1\ \mu\text{F},$$ so there is only one valid solution.

$$s^2+\frac{R}{L}s+\frac{1}{LC}=0$$ $$\frac{R}{L}=2\zeta\omega_0=150\times10^3,\qquad \frac{1}{LC}=\omega_0^2=2.5\times10^9$$ $$\boxed{R=600\ \Omega,\quad L=4\ \text{mH}}$$
Quick Data
Target Damping
$$\zeta=1.5$$
Target Frequency
$$\omega_0=50\times10^3\ \text{rad/s}$$
Fixed Capacitor
$$C=0.1\ \mu\text{F}=10^{-7}\ \text{F}$$
Solved Inductor
$$L=4\ \text{mH}$$
Solved Resistor
$$R=600\ \Omega$$
Design Freedom
None once $$C$$ is fixed
Answer Board
Constraint 1
$$R/L=150\times10^3$$
Constraint 2
$$1/(LC)=2.5\times10^9$$
Unique Answer
$$\boxed{R=600\ \Omega,\ L=4\ \text{mH}}$$
Rooms

Room 1: Match the Series Standard Form

  • Use $$s^2+\frac{R}{L}s+\frac{1}{LC}=0.$$
  • Compare with $$s^2+2\zeta\omega_0s+\omega_0^2=0.$$
  • Why: Because the standard form is the fastest way to turn target damping data into equations on the physical components.

Room 2: Convert the Design Targets

  • $$R/L=2\zeta\omega_0=2(1.5)(50\times10^3)=150\times10^3.$$
  • $$1/(LC)=\omega_0^2=(50\times10^3)^2=2.5\times10^9.$$
  • Why: Because design targets such as zeta and omega0 are useful only after they are rewritten as coefficient constraints.

Room 3: Use the Fixed Capacitor

  • The capacitor is given: $$C=0.1\ \mu\text{F}=10^{-7}\ \text{F}.$$
  • This removes the free-choice step that appears in the open design examples.
  • Why: Because fixing one storage element removes the free-choice degree of freedom and turns the design into a unique solve.

Room 4: Solve for the Inductor

  • $$L=1/(C\omega_0^2)=1/((10^{-7})(2.5\times10^9))=4\times10^{-3}\ \text{H}=4\ \text{mH}.$$
  • Why: Because the fixed-capacitor condition leaves inductance as the next element determined by the frequency constraint.

Room 5: Solve for the Resistor

  • Now use $$R/L=150\times10^3.$$
  • $$R=(150\times10^3)(4\times10^{-3})=600\ \Omega.$$
  • Why: Because once the energy-storage terms are fixed, the damping equation leaves the resistor as the final value to lock.

Room 6: Why the Answer Is Unique

  • With $$C$$ fixed, the two equations determine $$L$$ and $$R$$ exactly.
  • There is no remaining design freedom.
  • Why: Because the final conceptual check should explain not just the numbers, but why no alternate element pair can satisfy the same fully constrained setup.

Fast Memory Rule

When one energy-storage component is fixed, design freedom disappears. Use $$\omega_0$$ to solve the other storage element and $$\zeta$$ to solve the resistor.

Memory Palace Path
