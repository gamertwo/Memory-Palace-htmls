Example 7-21

The switch in Figure 7-40 has been open for a long time and closes at $$t=0.$$ Find the initial conditions, the inductor current, and the capacitor voltage for $$t\ge 0.$$ The post-switch network is a zero-input parallel RLC with unequal real roots.

$$v_C(0)=0,\qquad i_L(0)=\frac{9}{250+50}=30\ \text{mA}$$ $$i_L(t)=30.3e^{-50.51t}-0.309e^{-4950t}\ \text{mA},\qquad v_C(t)=-1.53e^{-50.51t}+1.53e^{-4950t}\ \text{V}$$
Quick Data
Initial Voltage
$$v_C(0)=0$$
Initial Current
$$i_L(0)=30\ \text{mA}$$
Parallel Elements
$$R=50\ \Omega,\ L=1\ \text{H},\ C=4\ \mu\text{F}$$
Characteristic Eq.
$$LCs^2+\frac{L}{R}s+1=0$$
Roots
$$s_1=-50.51,\quad s_2=-4950$$
Case
Overdamped: two real unequal roots
Answer Board
Part (a)
$$v_C(0)=0,\qquad i_L(0)=30\ \text{mA}$$
Part (b)
$$i_L(t)=30.3e^{-50.51t}-0.309e^{-4950t}\ \text{mA}$$
Part (c)
$$v_C(t)=-1.53e^{-50.51t}+1.53e^{-4950t}\ \text{V}$$
Switch Current
Use $$i_{sw}(t)=i_{50}(t)+i_{250}(t)$$ with $$i_{50}=\dfrac{v_C(t)}{50}$$ and $$i_{250}=\dfrac{9-v_C(t)}{250}.$$
Rooms

Room 1: Read the Initial Conditions

  • Before switching, the circuit is in steady state.
  • The inductor behaves like a short circuit and the capacitor behaves like an open circuit.
  • That gives
  • $$v_C(0)=0,\qquad i_L(0)=\frac{9}{250+50}=30\ \text{mA}. $$

Room 2: Write the Parallel Characteristic Equation

  • For the zero-input parallel RLC network, use
  • $$LCs^2+\frac{L}{R}s+1=0.$$
  • With $$L=1,$$ $$C=4\times 10^{-6},$$ and $$R=50,$$ the roots are
  • $$s_1=-50.51,\qquad s_2=-4950.$$

Room 3: Pick the Overdamped Template

  • Because the roots are real and unequal, the inductor current is
  • $$i_L(t)=K_1e^{-50.51t}+K_2e^{-4950t}. $$

Room 4: Apply $$i_L(0)$$

  • At $$t=0,$$
  • $$K_1+K_2=30\times 10^{-3}. $$

Room 5: Convert $$v_C(0)=0$$ into a Derivative Condition

  • Use the inductor voltage relation
  • $$v_C(0)=v_L(0)=L\left.\frac{di_L}{dt}\right|_0=0.$$
  • So
  • $$-50.51K_1-4950K_2=0.$$

Room 6: Solve for the Constants

  • Solving the two equations gives
  • $$K_1=30.3\ \text{mA},\qquad K_2=-0.309\ \text{mA}. $$
  • Therefore
  • $$\boxed{i_L(t)=30.3e^{-50.51t}-0.309e^{-4950t}\ \text{mA}}.$$

Room 7: Differentiate to Get the Capacitor Voltage

  • Use $$v_C(t)=L\,di_L/dt.$$
  • That gives
  • $$\boxed{v_C(t)=-1.53e^{-50.51t}+1.53e^{-4950t}\ \text{V}}.$$

Room 8: Recover the Switch Current

  • The switch current is the sum of the currents in the two resistor branches attached to the switch node.
  • Write
  • $$i_{sw}(t)=i_{50}(t)+i_{250}(t),$$
  • then substitute $$i_{50}=v_C/50$$ and $$i_{250}=(9-v_C)/250.$$

Fast Memory Rule

This example is a standard switch-to-zero-input move: read the initial current and voltage from the pre-switch steady state, solve the post-switch parallel characteristic equation, get $$i_L(t),$$ then differentiate once to recover $$v_C(t).$$

Memory Palace Path

The route starts in the pre-switch steady state, then drops into the post-switch zero-input parallel RLC skeleton.

Station 1: Steady-State Gate

Use the inductor short and capacitor open to read $$v_C(0)$$ and $$i_L(0).$$

Transition 1 to 2

Those two initial values feed the zero-input response immediately.

Station 2: Root Hall

Write the parallel characteristic equation and keep the two real roots.

Transition 2 to 3

The roots force the overdamped current template.

Station 3: Current Lock

Use $$i_L(0)$$ and $$v_C(0)=L di_L/dt|_0$$ to solve $$K_1$$ and $$K_2.$$

Transition 3 to 4

Once the inductor current is fixed, the capacitor voltage is only one derivative away.

Station 4: Voltage Exit

Differentiate once for $$v_C(t),$$ then use resistor currents to recover the switch current.
