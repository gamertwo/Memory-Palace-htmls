The One Skeleton
Every first-order circuit page on this site reduces to the same backbone: $$x(t)=x(\infty)+\big[x(0)-x(\infty)\big]e^{-t/\tau}$$ Your job is always the same: identify the state variable, freeze the initial value, melt the final value, get $\tau$, and then rebuild the response or the circuit.
🏛 The 5-Move Algorithm
I · Recognize
What family is this?
Decide if the waveform or circuit is RC or RL, and pick the correct state variable first.
II · Freeze
Read the initial value
Use the pre-switch steady-state circuit. Capacitor voltage and inductor current cannot jump.
III · Melt
Read the final value
Use the long-time post-switch circuit where the exponential has died out.
IV · Flip
Get the time constant
Read the exponent coefficient directly or compute $\tau = RC$ / $\tau = L/R$ from the post-switch circuit.
V · Build
Write the answer
Assemble the response, recover the ODE, or reverse-engineer the design depending on the problem type.

🧠 How To Use This
Study Workflow
Open a page, walk the rooms in order, and do not skip the circuit-reading step. For given-response pages, read the waveform first. For switching pages, derive the initial and final values from the circuit. For design pages, run the logic backward from the target waveform.
