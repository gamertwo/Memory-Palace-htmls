Example 7-16

This example mixes two ideas at once: a switching circuit and a sinusoidal forcing term. The clean way through it is to simplify the source side into a Thevenin equivalent first, then solve the RC equation using a natural exponential plus a sine-cosine forced part. The real lesson is not just the final formula, but why the forced response needs both sine and cosine terms even though the source shows only sine.

$$v(t)=4e^{-500t}-4\cos(1000t)+2\sin(1000t)\ \text{V},\qquad t\ge 0$$
Quick Data
Source
$$v_S(t)=20\sin(1000t)\,u(t)\ \text{V}$$
Resistors
$$4\,\text{k}\Omega,\ 4\,\text{k}\Omega$$
Capacitor
$$C=1\,\mu\text{F}$$
Initial State
Switch open for long time, so $$v(0)=0.$$
Why cosine appears
Differentiating sine creates cosine, so the forced-response trial form must include both terms.
Rooms

Room 1: Find the Thevenin Voltage Seen by the Capacitor

  • Use voltage division between equal $$4\,\text{k}\Omega$$ resistors:
  • $$v_T(t)=\frac{4}{4+4}v_S(t)=\frac{1}{2}\cdot 20\sin(1000t)=10\sin(1000t)\ \text{V}.$$

Room 2: Find Thevenin Resistance and Time Constant

  • With source turned off, the two $$4\,\text{k}\Omega$$ resistors are in parallel:
  • $$R_T=4\,\text{k}\Omega \parallel 4\,\text{k}\Omega=2\,\text{k}\Omega.$$
  • So
  • $$\tau=R_TC=(2\times10^3)(1\times10^{-6})=2\times10^{-3}\ \text{s}=\frac{1}{500}\ \text{s}.$$

Room 3: Write the Differential Equation

  • The first-order RC equation becomes
  • $$2\times10^{-3}\frac{dv(t)}{dt}+v(t)=10\sin(1000t),\qquad t\ge 0.$$

Room 4: Write Natural and Forced Forms

  • Natural part:
  • $$v_N(t)=Ke^{-500t}.$$
  • Forced guess (sinusoidal input):
  • $$v_F(t)=a\cos(1000t)+b\sin(1000t).$$
  • Use both terms because one trigonometric function turns into the other under differentiation.

Room 5: Substitute Forced Form and Collect Terms

  • Substituting $$v_F$$ gives
  • $$2\times10^{-3}\!\left[-1000a\sin(1000t)+1000b\cos(1000t)\right]+a\cos(1000t)+b\sin(1000t)=10\sin(1000t).$$
  • Collecting cosine and sine terms:
  • $$(a+2b)\cos(1000t)+(-2a+b-10)\sin(1000t)=0.$$

Room 6: Solve for $a$ and $b$

  • From coefficient matching:
  • $$a+2b=0,\qquad -2a+b=10.$$
  • So
  • $$a=-4,\qquad b=2.$$
  • Hence
  • $$v_F(t)=-4\cos(1000t)+2\sin(1000t).$$
  • This is only the steady forced part. The natural exponential still has to be added to satisfy the actual switching initial condition.

Room 7: Build Total Response and Apply Initial Condition

  • Total response:
  • $$v(t)=Ke^{-500t}-4\cos(1000t)+2\sin(1000t).$$
  • At $$t=0,$$
  • $$v(0)=K-4.$$
  • Given zero initial capacitor voltage, $$v(0)=0,$$ so $$K=4.$$
  • The natural term is what adjusts the source-driven waveform so the total response starts from the correct initial value.

Room 8: Final Answer

  • $$\boxed{v(t)=4e^{-500t}-4\cos(1000t)+2\sin(1000t)\ \text{V}},\qquad t\ge 0.$$
  • Equivalent amplitude-phase form for the sinusoidal part:
  • $$-4\cos(1000t)+2\sin(1000t)=4.47\cos(1000t-153.4^\circ).$$
  • So
  • $$v(t)=4e^{-500t}+4.47\cos(1000t-153.4^\circ)\ \text{V},\qquad t\ge 0.$$

Concept Summary

The source is sinusoidal, but the total response is not just some sine. It is a forced sine-cosine combination plus a natural exponential that corrects the waveform to satisfy the switching initial condition at $$t=0.$$

Key Shortcut

Fourier-Coefficient Guess

For sinusoidal forcing in first-order circuits, the fast path is: keep the natural exponential, guess a forced combination of cosine and sine, solve the two linear equations for coefficients, then apply the initial condition to fix the natural constant.

Memory Palace Path

This route stays strict: Thevenin first, time constant second, coefficient matching third, and initial condition last.

Station 1: Divider Gate

Shrink the source side to $$v_T(t)=10\sin(1000t).$$

Transition 1 to 2

After the Thevenin voltage, the capacitor still needs the resistance it sees.

Station 2: Parallel Bridge

Store $$R_T=2\,\text{k}\Omega$$ and $$\tau=1/500\ \text{s}.$$

Transition 2 to 3

Once $$\tau$$ is known, the first-order differential equation is immediate.

Station 3: ODE Hall

Write $$2\times10^{-3}\dot v+v=10\sin(1000t).$$

Transition 3 to 4

The input is sinusoidal, so the forced guess must carry both sine and cosine pieces.

Station 4: Coefficient Lab

Use $$a\cos(1000t)+b\sin(1000t)$$ and match coefficients.

Transition 4 to 5

After solving for $$a$$ and $$b$$, only the natural constant is unknown.

Station 5: Initial Lock

Apply $$v(0)=0$$ to get $$K=4.$$

Transition 5 to 6

With all constants fixed, only the final cleaned expression remains.

Station 6: Final Vault

Seal $$v(t)=4e^{-500t}-4\cos(1000t)+2\sin(1000t).$$
