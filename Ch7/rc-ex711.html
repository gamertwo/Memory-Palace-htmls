$$i_L(t)=0.5\left(1-e^{-5\times10^6 t}\right)\text{ mA},\quad t\ge 0$$
Quick Data
Clue 1
If you see $A(1-e^{-t/\tau})$, think "starts at zero, rises to $A$."
Clue 2
The number outside the parentheses is the final value.
Clue 3
The exponent coefficient is the reciprocal of the time constant.
Clue 4
For this pattern, the ODE is always $\tau \dot x + x = X_{\infty}$.
Rooms

Room 1: Recognize the Pattern

  • $i_L(t)=0.5(1-e^{-5\times10^6 t})$ matches the zero-state RL charging form $A(1-e^{-t/\tau})$.
  • So the inductor current starts at $0$ and rises toward $A$.

Room 2: Read the Final (Target) Current

  • Compare $i_L(t)=A(1-e^{-t/\tau})$ with $i_L(t)=0.5(1-e^{-5\times10^6 t})$.
  • Therefore $A=i_L(\infty)=0.5\text{ mA}$.

Room 3: Extract the Time Constant $\tau$

  • The exponent is $-5\times10^6 t$.
  • Match $e^{-t/\tau}$ with $e^{-5\times10^6 t}$, so $\frac{1}{\tau}=5\times10^6$.
  • $$\tau=\frac{1}{5\times10^6}=2\times10^{-7}\text{ s}=200\text{ ns}.$$

Room 4: Check Initial and Steady-State Values

  • At $t=0$: $$i_L(0)=0.5(1-e^0)=0.5(1-1)=0\text{ mA}.$$
  • As $t\to\infty$: $e^{-5\times10^6 t}\to 0$, so $$i_L(\infty)=0.5(1-0)=0.5\text{ mA}.$$

Room 5: Split Into Forced + Natural Parts

  • Expand: $$i_L(t)=0.5-0.5e^{-5\times10^6 t}\text{ mA}.$$
  • Forced part = $0.5\text{ mA}$.
  • Natural part = $-0.5e^{-5\times10^6 t}\text{ mA}$.

Room 6: Rebuild the Governing First-Order ODE

  • Standard first-order RL form: $$\tau\frac{di_L}{dt}+i_L=i_L(\infty)u(t).$$
  • Substitute $\tau=2\times10^{-7}$ and $i_L(\infty)=0.5$.
  • $$2\times10^{-7}\frac{di_L}{dt}+i_L=0.5u(t),\quad t\ge 0.$$

Room 7: Quick Verification

  • Differentiate: $$\frac{di_L}{dt}=0.5\left(5\times10^6\right)e^{-5\times10^6 t}=2.5\times10^6 e^{-5\times10^6 t}.$$
  • Substitute into the left side:
  • $$2\times10^{-7}\left(2.5\times10^6 e^{-5\times10^6 t}\right)+0.5\left(1-e^{-5\times10^6 t}\right)=0.5e^{-5\times10^6 t}+0.5-0.5e^{-5\times10^6 t}=0.5.$$
  • So the left side matches the right side $0.5u(t)$ for $t>0$.
