$$v_C(t)=2.5\left(1-e^{-200t}\right),\quad t\ge 0$$
Quick Data
Clue 1
If you see $A(1-e^{-t/\tau})$, think "starts at zero, rises to $A$."
Clue 2
The number outside the parentheses is the final value.
Clue 3
The exponent coefficient is the reciprocal of the time constant.
Clue 4
For this pattern, the ODE is always $\tau \dot v + v = V_{\infty}$.
Rooms

Room 1: Recognize the Pattern

  • $v_C(t)=2.5(1-e^{-200t})$ matches the zero-state RC charging form $A(1-e^{-t/\tau})$.
  • So the capacitor starts at $0\text{ V}$ and rises toward $A$.

Room 2: Read the Final (Target) Voltage

  • Compare $v_C(t)=A(1-e^{-t/\tau})$ with $v_C(t)=2.5(1-e^{-200t})$.
  • Therefore $A=v_C(\infty)=2.5\text{ V}$.

Room 3: Extract the Time Constant $\tau$

  • The exponent is $-200t$.
  • Match $e^{-t/\tau}$ with $e^{-200t}$, so $\frac{1}{\tau}=200$.
  • $$\tau=\frac{1}{200}=0.005\text{ s}=5\text{ ms}.$$

Room 4: Check Initial and Steady-State Values

  • At $t=0$: $$v_C(0)=2.5(1-e^0)=2.5(1-1)=0\text{ V}.$$
  • As $t\to\infty$: $e^{-200t}\to 0$, so $$v_C(\infty)=2.5(1-0)=2.5\text{ V}.$$

Room 5: Split Into Forced + Natural Parts

  • Expand: $$v_C(t)=2.5-2.5e^{-200t}.$$
  • Forced part = $2.5\text{ V}$.
  • Natural part = $-2.5e^{-200t}$.

Room 6: Rebuild the Governing First-Order ODE

  • Standard first-order RC form: $$\tau\frac{dv_C}{dt}+v_C=v_C(\infty)u(t).$$
  • Substitute $\tau=0.005$ and $v_C(\infty)=2.5$.
  • $$0.005\frac{dv_C}{dt}+v_C=2.5u(t),\quad t\ge 0.$$

Room 7: Quick Verification

  • Differentiate: $$\frac{dv_C}{dt}=500e^{-200t}.$$
  • Substitute into the left side:
  • $$0.005(500e^{-200t})+2.5(1-e^{-200t})=2.5e^{-200t}+2.5-2.5e^{-200t}=2.5.$$
  • So the left side matches the right side $2.5u(t)$ for $t>0$.
