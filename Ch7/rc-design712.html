Design Example 7-12

This is a design problem: instead of solving for the response from a circuit, you reverse-engineer the circuit values from the desired capacitor voltage.

$$v_C(t)=50-100e^{-2000t}\text{ V}$$
Quick Data
Clue 1
Design problems run the normal solve path backward: read the waveform first, then infer the circuit.
Clue 2
The constant term is the final value. The full expression at t=0 gives the initial value.
Clue 3
The exponent coefficient gives the time constant immediately by inversion.
Clue 4
Once you know tau, picking one standard part lets you solve for the other using tau = RC.
Rooms

Room 1: Recognize the Design Target

  • The desired response already has the standard first-order RC form.
  • So the job is to choose a source, switch, resistor, and capacitor that produce that exact charging curve.

Room 2: Read the Final Voltage

  • As $t\to\infty$, the exponential dies.
  • So $$v_C(\infty)=50\text{ V}.$$

Room 3: Read the Initial Voltage

  • At $t=0$, $e^0=1$.
  • So $$v_C(0)=50-100=-50\text{ V}.$$

Room 4: Extract the Time Constant

  • Match $e^{-t/\tau}$ with $e^{-2000t}$.
  • That gives $$\frac{1}{\tau}=2000 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \tau=\frac{1}{2000}=500\mu\text{s}.$$
  • For an RC circuit, $$\tau=RC.$$

Room 5: Choose Standard Parts

  • Select a standard capacitor value, for example $$C=0.015\mu\text{F}.$$
  • Then solve for the resistor:
  • $$R=\frac{\tau}{C}=\frac{500\mu\text{s}}{0.015\mu\text{F}}\approx 33.3k\Omega.$$
  • A standard value close to that is $$R=33k\Omega.$$

Room 6: Infer the Source Arrangement

  • The response moves from $-50\text{ V}$ to $+50\text{ V}$.
  • That suggests switching the capacitor node between two source conditions: one that establishes $-50\text{ V}$ initially and one that drives it toward $+50\text{ V}$ finally.
  • So the design needs a switch that flips the capacitor between those two polarity conditions.

Room 7: Rebuild the Governing ODE

  • The standard RC equation is
  • $$\tau\frac{dv_C}{dt}+v_C=v_C(\infty)u(t).$$
  • With $$\tau=500\mu\text{s} \quad \text{and} \quad v_C(\infty)=50,$$
  • the target equation is $$0.0005\frac{dv_C}{dt}+v_C=50u(t).$$

Room 8: Quick Verification

  • Differentiate the desired response:
  • $$\frac{dv_C}{dt}=200000e^{-2000t}.$$
  • Substitute into the left side:
  • $$0.0005(200000e^{-2000t})+\left(50-100e^{-2000t}\right)=100e^{-2000t}+50-100e^{-2000t}=50.$$
  • So the desired waveform matches the target first-order equation.
