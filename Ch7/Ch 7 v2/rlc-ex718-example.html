Example 7-18

Same circuit as 7-17 ($L=1\ \text{H},\ C=0.25\times10^{-6}\ \text{F}$), but now find the zero-input capacitor voltage after the switch closes at $t=0.$ Initial conditions: $I_0=0$ and $V_0=15\ \text{V}.$ Reuse the roots from 7-17, pick the correct template for each case, and solve for the two unknown constants.

🔑
The Master Law of This Example
Roots decide the shape of the response. Initial conditions decide the exact waveform. This two-step law is repeated for all three damping cases.
Roots from 7-17 → Choose Template → Apply ICs → Solve Constants → Write Waveform
Quick Data
Capacitance
$C=0.25\times10^{-6}\ \text{F}$
Inductance
$L=1\ \text{H}$
Initial Voltage
$v_C(0)=V_0=15\ \text{V}$
Initial Current
$I_0=0\ \Rightarrow\ \dot v_C(0)=0$
Roots (from 7-17)
$-500,-8000$ · $-2000,-2000$ · $-500\pm j500\sqrt{15}$
Goal
$v_C(t)$ for all three damping cases
The Two Keys — Initial Conditions
Same starting data for every case
These two equations are all you need to pin down the unknown constants $K_1$ and $K_2$ in any of the three response templates. They never change between cases — only the template changes.
Condition 1 — Voltage
$v_C(0) = 15\ \text{V}$
Plug $t=0$ into the template → equation for $K_1, K_2$
Condition 2 — Derivative
$\dot v_C(0) = I_0/C = 0$
Differentiate the template, plug $t=0$ → second equation
Why $\dot v_C(0) = 0$
$i_C = C\,\dfrac{dv_C}{dt}$
Since $I_0=0,$ the capacitor current is zero at $t=0$, so the derivative is zero
The Three Response Templates & Final Answers
🔩
Case A · Overdamped
Two Real Exponentials
Resistance$R = 8.5\ \text{k}\Omega$
$s_1 = -500$ $s_2 = -8000$ two distinct real roots
Response Template
$v_C = K_1 e^{-500t} + K_2 e^{-8000t}$
$K_1 = 16$ $K_2 = -1$
Final Answer
$v_C(t) = 16e^{-500t} - e^{-8000t}\ \text{V},\quad t \ge 0$
The slow term $e^{-500t}$ dominates the long tail. The fast term $e^{-8000t}$ vanishes almost instantly. No oscillation — two real decays side by side.
⚖️
Case B · Critically Damped
Repeated Root with $t$ Factor
Resistance$R = 4\ \text{k}\Omega$
$s = -2000\ (\times 2)$ one repeated real root
Response Template
$v_C = (K_1 + K_2\,t)\,e^{-2000t}$
$K_1 = 15$ $K_2 = 30000$
Final Answer
$v_C(t) = 15(1+2000t)\,e^{-2000t}\ \text{V},\quad t \ge 0$
The extra $t$ factor in the template is forced by the repeated root — it distinguishes this from two separate exponentials. Fastest return to zero without ringing.
🔔
Case C · Underdamped
Decaying Sinusoid
Resistance$R = 1\ \text{k}\Omega$
$-500 \pm j500\sqrt{15}$ complex conjugates
Response Template ($\omega_d = 500\sqrt{15}$)
$v_C = e^{-500t}(K_1\cos\omega_d t + K_2\sin\omega_d t)$
$K_1 = 15$ $K_2 = \sqrt{15}$
Final Answer
$v_C(t) = e^{-500t}\!\left[15\cos(500\sqrt{15}\,t)+\sqrt{15}\sin(500\sqrt{15}\,t)\right]\text{V}$
$e^{-500t}$ is the decaying envelope. The cosine and sine inside create the oscillation. Real part of root → decay. Imaginary part → oscillation frequency $\omega_d$.
Step-by-Step Algebra
1
Write the template for your case
Choose the form that matches the root type from Example 7-17. Don't start computing yet — the template comes first.
2
Apply Condition 1: set $t = 0$, equate to 15 V
Every exponential and every cosine/sine evaluates to a simple value at $t=0.$ This gives you one algebraic equation in $K_1$ and $K_2.$
3
Differentiate the template, then apply Condition 2: $\dot v_C(0) = 0$
Differentiate term by term, substitute $t=0,$ and set the result equal to zero. This gives you the second algebraic equation.
4
Solve the two equations for $K_1$ and $K_2$
Simple linear algebra. Substitute back into the template to get the final waveform.
1
Write the template
Roots are $s_1=-500$ and $s_2=-8000,$ so: $$v_C(t)=K_1e^{-500t}+K_2e^{-8000t}$$
2
Condition 1 — plug $t=0$
$$v_C(0)=K_1+K_2=15$$
3
Differentiate, then Condition 2 — set $\dot v_C(0) = 0$
$$\dot v_C = -500K_1e^{-500t} - 8000K_2e^{-8000t}$$ At $t=0:$ $$0 = -500K_1 - 8000K_2$$
4
Solve the system
From the two equations: $K_1+K_2=15$ and $500K_1+8000K_2=0.$ Solving: $K_1=16,\ K_2=-1.$
5
Write the final answer
$$v_C(t) = 16e^{-500t} - e^{-8000t}\ \text{V},\quad t\ge0$$
1
Write the repeated-root template
Both roots equal $-2000,$ so the extra $t$ factor is mandatory: $$v_C(t)=(K_1+K_2\,t)\,e^{-2000t}$$
2
Condition 1 — plug $t=0$
$$v_C(0)=K_1 \cdot 1 = 15 \quad\Rightarrow\quad K_1=15$$
3
Differentiate the template (product rule)
$$\dot v_C = K_2\,e^{-2000t} + (K_1+K_2\,t)(-2000)\,e^{-2000t}$$
4
Condition 2 — set $\dot v_C(0) = 0$
At $t=0:$ $$0 = K_2 - 2000K_1 = K_2 - 2000(15) \quad\Rightarrow\quad K_2=30000$$
5
Write the final answer
$$v_C(t)=15(1+2000t)\,e^{-2000t}\ \text{V},\quad t\ge 0$$ Note: $K_1+K_2\,t = 15+30000t = 15(1+2000t).$
1
Identify $\alpha$ and $\omega_d$ from roots
Roots are $-500\pm j500\sqrt{15},$ so $\alpha=500$ and $\omega_d=500\sqrt{15}.$
2
Write the underdamped template
$$v_C(t)=e^{-500t}\!\left(K_1\cos(500\sqrt{15}\,t)+K_2\sin(500\sqrt{15}\,t)\right)$$
3
Condition 1 — plug $t=0$
At $t=0:\ \cos(0)=1,\ \sin(0)=0,\ e^0=1:$ $$v_C(0) = K_1 = 15$$
4
Differentiate, then Condition 2 — set $\dot v_C(0) = 0$
Differentiate and evaluate at $t=0:$ $$0=-500K_1 + 500\sqrt{15}\,K_2$$ $$0=-500(15)+500\sqrt{15}\,K_2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad K_2=\sqrt{15}$$
5
Write the final answer
$$v_C(t)=e^{-500t}\!\left[15\cos(500\sqrt{15}\,t)+\sqrt{15}\sin(500\sqrt{15}\,t)\right]\text{V},\quad t\ge0$$
Memory Palace

This palace has one law burned into every room: roots pick the template — initial conditions pick the constants. Walk through a Template Hall, an IC Lock, and three Damping Doors. Each door has a feel: stone (heavy, real, separate), mirror (balanced, repeated), silver-ringing (oscillating, decaying). You never re-derive from scratch — you just choose the right door.

2
🔐 The Double IC Lock
A lock with exactly two keys, the same every time: $v_C(0)=15\ \text{V}$ and $\dot v_C(0)=0.$ No matter which door opens next, these two keys solve the constants. Same keys, different doors.
A

A heavy stone door. Stone = two separate real decays. Behind it: $K_1=16$ (slow, dominant arm) and $K_2=-1$ (fast, almost-invisible arm). Stamp: $16e^{-500t}-e^{-8000t}.$
↓
🌅 The Long-Tail Balcony
Step outside onto a balcony. The fast $e^{-8000t}$ disappears almost instantly, leaving only the slow tail $16e^{-500t}$ stretched across the horizon. Two rates, one survivor.
B
🪞 Mirror Door — Critically Damped ($R=4\ \text{k}\Omega$)
A mirror split perfectly down the center. Mirror = balance = repeated root. The extra $t$ multiplier in the template is the mirror's signature. Stamp: $15(1+2000t)e^{-2000t}.$
↓
🏃 The Narrowing Corridor
This corridor narrows sharply to zero as fast as physically possible, no ringing. Critical damping is the fastest return without oscillation. Knife edge between split and ring.
C
🔔 Silver Door — Underdamped ($R=1\ \text{k}\Omega$)
A silver door vibrating with a soft ring. Silver = oscillation. Inside: $e^{-500t}$ is the shrinking envelope; the cosine and sine create the oscillation. Stamp: $e^{-500t}[15\cos\omega_d t + \sqrt{15}\sin\omega_d t].$
∞
