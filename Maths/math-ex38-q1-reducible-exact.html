Exercise 3.8 Question 1

This equation is not exact at the start. The winning move is Rule II: compute the y-only ratio, build the integrating factor, multiply through, and then finish it as an exact equation.

$$(y\log y)\,dx+(x-\log y)\,dy=0$$

$$x\log y-\frac{(\log y)^2}{2}=C$$

$$2x\log y=C+(\log y)^2$$
Ex 3.8 GuideOpen in Memory Builder
Quick Data
M(x,y)
$$M=y\log y$$
N(x,y)
$$N=x-\log y$$
Rule Test
$$\frac{N_x-M_y}{M}=\frac{1-(1+\log y)}{y\log y}=-\frac1y$$
Integrating Factor
$$\text{I.F.}=e^{\int -1/y\,dy}=\frac1y$$
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Identify $M$ and $N$

  • Write the equation in standard form $$M\,dx+N\,dy=0.$$
  • Here $$M=y\log y$$ and $$N=x-\log y.$$
  • This is the raw form before any integrating factor is used.

Room 2: Show It Is Not Exact

  • Differentiate: $$M_y=1+\log y,\qquad N_x=1.$$
  • Since $$M_y\neq N_x,$$ the equation is not exact yet.

Room 3: Apply Rule II

  • Use $$\dfrac{N_x-M_y}{M}$$ because this one collapses to a function of $$y$$ only.
  • That gives $$\dfrac{1-(1+\log y)}{y\log y}=-\dfrac1y.$$
  • So the integrating factor is $$\text{I.F.}=e^{\int -1/y\,dy}=\dfrac1y.$$

Room 4: Multiply Through

  • Multiplying the whole equation by $$1/y$$ gives $$\log y\,dx+\left(\frac{x}{y}-\frac{\log y}{y}\right)dy=0.$$
  • This new equation is exact.

Room 5: Solve the Exact Equation

  • Integrate the new $$M=\log y$$ with respect to $$x$$: $$F=x\log y+g(y).$$
  • Differentiate with respect to $$y$$: $$F_y=\frac{x}{y}+g'(y).$$
  • Matching with $$N=\dfrac{x}{y}-\dfrac{\log y}{y}$$ gives $$g'(y)=-\dfrac{\log y}{y}.$$
  • Hence $$g(y)=-\dfrac{(\log y)^2}{2}.$$

Room 6: Final Answer

  • The final potential function is $$F=x\log y-\frac{(\log y)^2}{2}.$$
  • So $$x\log y-\frac{(\log y)^2}{2}=C.$$
  • Equivalently, $$2x\log y=C+(\log y)^2.$$

Key Shortcut

If the ratio becomes a function of y alone, switch to Rule II immediately instead of forcing Rule I.

Why This Flow Matters

This first question is the clean reminder that the integrating factor can depend on y rather than x.

Solution Flow

The pattern is stable: fail exactness, find the integrating factor, then solve the corrected exact form.

Identify→Not exact→Rule II→Multiply by I.F.→Solve exact form
