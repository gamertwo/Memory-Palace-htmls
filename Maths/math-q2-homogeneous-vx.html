Q2 � Homogeneous Example (y = vx)

Full method page with professional styling and room-based recall cues.

Question
$$(2xy+x^2)\frac{dy}{dx}=3y^2+2xy$$
Worked Solution
Step 1: Convert to ratio form: $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{3y^2+2xy}{2xy+x^2}$.
Step 2: Put $y=vx$, so $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=v+x\dfrac{dv}{dx}$.
$$v+x\frac{dv}{dx}=\frac{3v^2+2v}{2v+1}$$
Step 3: Rearrangement:
$$x\frac{dv}{dx}=\frac{v^2+v}{2v+1}\Rightarrow\frac{2v+1}{v^2+v}dv=\frac{dx}{x}$$
Step 4: Integrate:
$$\ln(v^2+v)=\ln x+\ln C\Rightarrow v^2+v=Cx$$
Step 5: Back-substitute $v=\dfrac{y}{x}$:
$$\frac{y^2}{x^2}+\frac{y}{x}=Cx\Rightarrow y^2+xy=Cx^3$$
Final Answer
$$\boxed{y^2+xy=Cx^3}$$
