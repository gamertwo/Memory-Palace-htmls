Exercise 3.13 Odd Route

Question 1

The equation is not a true Clairaut form. The useful recognition is that it becomes homogeneous after dividing by $x$, so we move into the substitution zone $y=vx$ and solve the reduced separable equation with every algebra step shown.

$$xp^2+x=2yp \qquad \text{where } p=\frac{dy}{dx}$$

$$2cy=c^2x^2+1$$

Room 1 Actual Explanation: Read The Equation Correctly

Problem: Solve $$xp^2+x=2yp$$ where $$p=\dfrac{dy}{dx}.$$

Type / Recognition: This is algebraic in $$p,$$ but the coefficients contain $$x$$ and $$y$$ in the same degree pattern. Divide by $$x$$ first so the equation can be written using $$y/x,$$ which signals the homogeneous substitution $$y=vx.$$

Starting equation:

$$xp^2+x=2yp$$

Since every term contains either an explicit factor of $$x$$ or a factor of $$y$$ multiplied by $$p,$$ divide both sides by $$x$$. This is valid as part of the ordinary differential equation manipulation on the nonzero branch where the solution is being built:

$$p^2+1=2\frac{y}{x}p$$

Now the ratio $$\dfrac{y}{x}$$ appears. That is the main signal for using $$y=vx,$$ because then $$\dfrac{y}{x}=v$$ becomes a single new variable.

Room 2 Actual Explanation: Substitute $$y=vx$$ Carefully

Let

$$y=vx$$

Differentiate both sides with respect to $$x$$. Because $$v$$ depends on $$x$$, use the product rule:

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=v+x\frac{dv}{dx}$$

But $$p=\dfrac{dy}{dx},$$ so

$$p=v+x\frac{dv}{dx}$$

Substitute $$\dfrac{y}{x}=v$$ and $$p=v+x\dfrac{dv}{dx}$$ into $$p^2+1=2\dfrac{y}{x}p$$:

$$\left(v+x\frac{dv}{dx}\right)^2+1=2v\left(v+x\frac{dv}{dx}\right)$$

This substitution is chosen because it converts the mixed appearance of $$x$$ and $$y$$ into a single ratio variable $$v.$$

Room 3 Actual Explanation: Expand And Rearrange Without Skipping

Expand the square on the left:

$$v^2+2vx\frac{dv}{dx}+x^2\left(\frac{dv}{dx}\right)^2+1=2v^2+2vx\frac{dv}{dx}$$

Subtract $$2vx\dfrac{dv}{dx}$$ from both sides. Those terms cancel:

$$v^2+x^2\left(\frac{dv}{dx}\right)^2+1=2v^2$$

Now subtract $$v^2+1$$ from both sides:

$$x^2\left(\frac{dv}{dx}\right)^2=v^2-1$$

Take square roots of both sides, which creates two branches:

$$x\frac{dv}{dx}=\pm\sqrt{v^2-1}$$

The plus-minus appears because both positive and negative square roots satisfy the squared equation.

Room 4 Actual Explanation: Separate And Integrate

Move all $$v$$ terms to one side and all $$x$$ terms to the other:

$$\frac{dv}{\sqrt{v^2-1}}=\pm\frac{dx}{x}$$

This is separable because the left side now depends only on $$v$$ and the right side only on $$x.$$ Integrate both sides:

$$\int \frac{dv}{\sqrt{v^2-1}}=\pm\int \frac{dx}{x}$$

The standard antiderivative is

$$\int \frac{dv}{\sqrt{v^2-1}}=\ln\left|v+\sqrt{v^2-1}\right|$$

So we get

$$\ln\left|v+\sqrt{v^2-1}\right|=\pm\ln|x|+C$$

Exponentiating and absorbing sign choices into the constant gives the standard equivalent form

$$v=\frac12\left(cx+\frac1{cx}\right)$$

Room 5 Actual Explanation: Return To $$y$$ And Match The Book Answer

Since $$y=vx,$$ multiply the expression for $$v$$ by $$x$$:

$$y=x\cdot \frac12\left(cx+\frac1{cx}\right)$$

Distribute $$x$$ across both terms:

$$y=\frac12\left(cx^2+\frac1c\right)$$

Multiply both sides by $$2$$:

$$2y=cx^2+\frac1c$$

Now rename the constant by multiplying through by $$c$$, which is a standard constant cleanup step. That gives the textbook form:

$$2cy=c^2x^2+1$$
Memory Palace Rooms

Room 1: Ratio Gate In The Homogeneous Hall

The corridor opens with a bronze equation wall: $$xp^2+x=2yp.$$ Ichigo notices every major block carries the same $$x$$-weight, so he cuts the whole wall by one vertical $$x$$-blade. The wall shrinks into $$p^2+1=2(y/x)p.$$ That exposed ratio $$y/x$$ glows like a doorway, signaling the substitution zone.

Room 2: The Substitution Bridge

Inside the next room, the $$y$$ tower folds into a stacked object $$vx.$$ A derivative slash passes through it and splits it into two pieces: a plain $$v$$ core and a moving bridge piece $$x\,dv/dx.$$ Together they become the new slope symbol $$p=v+x\,dv/dx.$$ The whole bridge locks into the equation frame.

Room 3: Expansion And Cancellation Forge

The squared bracket opens like a metal shell: $$\left(v+x\,dv/dx\right)^2.$$ Two identical middle gears appear on both sides and cancel each other exactly. What remains cools into one heavy block: $$x^2(dv/dx)^2=v^2-1.$$ Then the block is split by a root hammer into two exits: $$x\,dv/dx=+\sqrt{v^2-1}$$ and $$x\,dv/dx=-\sqrt{v^2-1}.$$

Room 4: Separation Stairway

Ichigo pulls all $$v$$ parts to the left railing and all $$x$$ parts to the right railing, forming $$dv/\sqrt{v^2-1}=\pm dx/x.$$ He walks both rails at once. The left rail becomes a hyperbolic log gate, and the right rail becomes a logarithm pillar. Their meeting crystallizes into $$v=\frac12\left(cx+\frac1{cx}\right).$$

Room 5: Final Frozen Image

The $$v$$ mask is removed and the original $$y=vx$$ body returns. Multiplying by $$x$$ stretches the image into $$y=\frac12\left(cx^2+\frac1c\right).$$ Then a constant-cleanup seal stamps the final frozen answer across the chamber:

$$2cy=c^2x^2+1$$
