Exercise 3.8 Question 5

The Rule I ratio is again $$-2/x,$$ so the integrating factor is $$x^{-2}.$$ After that, the exact answer finishes with a logarithm in $$x.$$

$$(x-y^2)\,dx+2xy\,dy=0$$

$$\log x+\frac{y^2}{x}=C$$

$$y^2=Cx-x\log x$$
Ex 3.8 GuideOpen in Memory Builder
Quick Data
M(x,y)
$$M=x-y^2$$
N(x,y)
$$N=2xy$$
Rule Test
$$\frac{M_y-N_x}{N}=\frac{-2y-2y}{2xy}=-\frac2x$$
Integrating Factor
$$\text{I.F.}=e^{\int -2/x\,dx}=\frac1{x^2}$$
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Identify $M$ and $N$

  • Take $$M=x-y^2$$ and $$N=2xy.$$

Room 2: Show It Is Not Exact

  • Compute $$M_y=-2y$$ and $$N_x=2y.$$
  • Since these are different, the equation is not exact.

Room 3: Apply Rule I

  • Use $$\dfrac{M_y-N_x}{N}.$$
  • It simplifies directly to $$-\dfrac2x.$$
  • So $$\text{I.F.}=e^{\int -2/x\,dx}=\dfrac1{x^2}.$$

Room 4: Multiply Through

  • Multiplying by $$x^{-2}$$ gives
  • $$\left(\frac1x-\frac{y^2}{x^2}\right)dx+\frac{2y}{x}\,dy=0,$$ which is exact.

Room 5: Solve the Exact Equation

  • Integrate the new $$M$$ with respect to $$x$$:
  • $$F=\log x+\frac{y^2}{x}+g(y).$$
  • Then $$F_y=\dfrac{2y}{x}+g'(y).$$ Matching with $$N=\dfrac{2y}{x}$$ gives $$g'(y)=0.$$

Room 6: Final Answer

  • The potential equation is $$\log x+\dfrac{y^2}{x}=C.$$
  • Equivalently, $$y^2=Cx-x\log x.$$

Key Shortcut

After multiplying by $x^{-2}$, integrate the two x-terms separately: $\int 1/x\,dx$ and $\int -y^2/x^2\,dx$.

Why This Flow Matters

This last question is a good bridge between integrating-factor work and a final answer that is rearranged into a cleaner algebraic form.

Solution Flow

This is the clean log-finish version of the same Rule I idea.

Identify→Not exact→Rule I gives $$-2/x$$→Multiply by $$x^{-2}$$→Exact + rearrange
