Question
$$\frac{dx}{x}=\tan y\,dy$$
Worked Solution
Step 1: Integrate both sides: $\int\frac{dx}{x}=\int\tan y\,dy$.
Step 2: Evaluate: $\ln|x|=-\ln|\cos y|+C$.
Step 3: Merge logs: $\ln|x|+\ln|\cos y|=C\Rightarrow\ln|x\cos y|=C$.
Step 4: Exponentiate and absorb constant: $x\cos y=C$.
Final Answer
$$\boxed{x\cos y=C}$$
