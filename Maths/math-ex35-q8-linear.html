Exercise 3.5 Question 8

Divide first by $$x\log x$$. That exposes the classic integrating-factor fingerprint $$\frac{1}{x\log x},$$ so the integrating factor becomes $$\log x.$$

$$x\log x\frac{dy}{dx}+y=2\log x\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \boxed{y\log x=(\log x)^2+C}$$
Quick Data
Standard Form
$$y'+\frac{1}{x\log x}y=\frac{2}{x}$$
Integrating Factor
$$\mu(x)=e^{\int dx/(x\log x)}=\log x$$
Product Form
$$\frac{d}{dx}(y\log x)=\frac{2\log x}{x}$$
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Normalize

  • Divide by $$x\log x$$:
  • $$\frac{dy}{dx}+\frac{1}{x\log x}y=\frac{2}{x}.$$

Room 2: Integrating Factor

  • $$P(x)=\frac{1}{x\log x}$$ so
  • $$\mu(x)=e^{\int dx/(x\log x)}=\log x.$$

Room 3: Multiply Through

  • Then
  • $$\frac{d}{dx}(y\log x)=\frac{2\log x}{x}.$$

Room 4: Integrate

  • $$y\log x=\int \frac{2\log x}{x}\,dx=(\log x)^2+C.$$

Room 5: Final Answer

  • Hence $$\boxed{y\log x=(\log x)^2+C}.$$

Key Shortcut

Whenever $$1/(x\log x)$$ appears, think of $$\log x$$ immediately. That identifies the integrating factor before any long work.
