Q1. $\frac{dx}{x}=\tan y\,dy$

$$\int\frac{dx}{x}=\int\tan y\,dy\Rightarrow \ln|x|=-\ln|\cos y|+C\Rightarrow x\cos y=C$$
Final: $\boxed{x\cos y=C}$
Room Cue
Split Gate: x and y already on separate sides.
Sign Cue
Tangent Lab: minus sign in $\int\tan y\,dy$.
Finish Cue
Constant Vault: merge logs, exponentiate, rename constant.

Q2. $(2xy+x^2)\dfrac{dy}{dx}=3y^2+2xy$

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{3y^2+2xy}{2xy+x^2},\;y=vx,\;\frac{dy}{dx}=v+x\frac{dv}{dx}$$ $$v+x\frac{dv}{dx}=\frac{3v^2+2v}{2v+1}\Rightarrow\frac{2v+1}{v^2+v}dv=\frac{dx}{x}$$ $$\ln(v^2+v)=\ln x+\ln C\Rightarrow v^2+v=Cx\Rightarrow y^2+xy=Cx^3$$
Final: $\boxed{y^2+xy=Cx^3}$
Room Cue
Degree Scanner: equal degree triggers homogeneous method.
Switch Cue
Substitution Switch: instantly set $y=vx$.
Back-Sub Cue
Back-Sub Chamber: restore $v=y/x$ at end.

Q3. $\frac{dx}{x}=\cot y\,dy$

$$\ln|x|=\ln|\sin y|+C\Rightarrow \frac{x}{\sin y}=C$$
Final: $\boxed{\frac{x}{\sin y}=C}$
Identity Cue
Sin Log Room: cot maps to $\ln|\sin y|$.
Compression Cue
Ratio Finish: log subtraction becomes quotient.

Q4. $\frac{dx}{x}=\sec y\tan y\,dy$

$$\ln|x|=\sec y + C\Rightarrow x=Ce^{\sec y}$$
Final: $\boxed{x=Ce^{\sec y}}$
Derivative Cue
Derivative Lock: sec-tan is derivative fingerprint of sec.
Finish Cue
Log-Exp Bridge: from ln form to exponential final.

Q5. $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{\sqrt{1-y^2}}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$

$$\frac{dy}{\sqrt{1-y^2}}=\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\Rightarrow \sin^{-1}y=\sin^{-1}x+C$$
Final: $\boxed{\sin^{-1}y=\sin^{-1}x+C}$
Identity Cue
Arcsin Corridor: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-u^2}}$ always maps to $\sin^{-1}u$.
Mirror Cue
Symmetry Hall: both sides have same arcsin structure.

Q6. $y\sqrt{1+x^2}\,dy+x\sqrt{1+y^2}\,dx=0$

$$\frac{y}{\sqrt{1+y^2}}dy+\frac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}dx=0$$ $$\Rightarrow \sqrt{1+y^2}+\sqrt{1+x^2}=C$$
Final: $\boxed{\sqrt{1+y^2}+\sqrt{1+x^2}=C}$
Prep Cue
Twin Root Walls: divide by both radicals first.
Integral Cue
Root Lift: $u/\sqrt{1+u^2}$ integrates to $\sqrt{1+u^2}$.

Q7 (3.2 Q4). $\sec^2 x\tan y\,dx+\sec^2 y\tan x\,dy=0$

$$\frac{\sec^2 x}{\tan x}dx+\frac{\sec^2 y}{\tan y}dy=0$$ $$\Rightarrow \ln|\tan x|+\ln|\tan y|=C\Rightarrow \tan x\tan y=C$$
Final: $\boxed{\tan x\tan y=C}$
Cancel Cue
Tan-Cancel Room: divide by $\tan x\tan y$ first.
Derivative Cue
Log-Tan Signature: $\sec^2u/\tan u=d(\ln|\tan u|)/du$.

Q8 (3.2 Q5). $(1+x^2)dy-xy\,dx=0$

$$(1+x^2)dy=xy\,dx\Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{x}{1+x^2}y$$ $$\Rightarrow \frac{dy}{y}=\frac{x}{1+x^2}dx$$ $$\Rightarrow \ln|y|=\frac12\ln(1+x^2)+C\Rightarrow y=C\sqrt{1+x^2}$$
Final: $\boxed{y=C\sqrt{1+x^2}}$
Separation Cue
Balance Board: isolate dy and dx terms before integrating.
Integral Cue
Half-Log Chamber: $\int x/(1+x^2)\,dx=\frac12\ln(1+x^2)$.

Q9. $\left(e^y+1\right)\cos x\,dx + e^y\sin x\,dy = 0$

$$\cos x\,dx \Rightarrow \sin x,\qquad e^y\,dy \Rightarrow e^y+1$$ $$\Rightarrow \text{candidate product } \left(e^y+1\right)\sin x$$ $$d\!\left[\left(e^y+1\right)\sin x\right]=\left(e^y+1\right)\cos x\,dx+e^y\sin x\,dy$$ $$\Rightarrow d\!\left[\left(e^y+1\right)\sin x\right]=0\Rightarrow \left(e^y+1\right)\sin x=C$$
Final: $\boxed{\left(e^y+1\right)\sin x=C}$
Trigger Cue
Product Detector: both terms fit one product-rule derivative.
Anchor Cue
Sine Anchor: $\cos x\,dx$ points directly to $\sin x$.
Finish Cue
Exact Collapse: once the full differential is seen, set it to zero and seal with $C$.

Q10. $3e^x\tan y\,dx + \left(1-e^x\right)\sec^2 y\,dy = 0$

$$u=1-e^x,\;du=-e^x\,dx$$ $$\Rightarrow -3\tan y\,du+u\sec^2 y\,dy=0$$ $$\Rightarrow -3\frac{du}{u}+\frac{\sec^2 y}{\tan y}dy=0$$ $$\Rightarrow -3\ln|u|+\ln|\tan y|=C$$ $$\Rightarrow \left(1-e^x\right)^3=C\tan y$$
Final: $\boxed{\left(1-e^x\right)^3=C\tan y}$
Sub Cue
Bracket Switch: set $u=1-e^x$ immediately.
Sign Cue
Minus Trap: $du=-e^x dx$ gives the sign you must keep.
Trig Cue
Log-Tan Lock: $\sec^2 y/\tan y$ integrates to $\ln|\tan y|$.

Q11. $\left(e^y+2\right)\sin x\,dx - e^y\cos x\,dy = 0$

$$\sin x\,dx \Rightarrow -\cos x,\qquad e^y\,dy \Rightarrow e^y+2$$ $$\Rightarrow \text{candidate } -\left(e^y+2\right)\cos x$$ $$d\!\left[-\left(e^y+2\right)\cos x\right]=\left(e^y+2\right)\sin x\,dx-e^y\cos x\,dy$$ $$\Rightarrow -\left(e^y+2\right)\cos x=C \Rightarrow \left(e^y+2\right)\cos x=C$$
Final: $\boxed{\left(e^y+2\right)\cos x=C}$
Sign Cue
Cos Flip: $\sin x\,dx$ points to $-\cos x$, not $\cos x$.
Bracket Cue
Exponential Shell: $e^y\,dy$ still points to the full bracket $e^y+$ constant.
Finish Cue
Constant Cleanup: absorb the overall minus sign into $C$ at the end.

Q12. $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=e^{x-y}+x^2e^{-y}$

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=e^{-y}(e^x+x^2)$$ $$\Rightarrow e^y\frac{dy}{dx}=e^x+x^2$$ $$\Rightarrow \frac{d}{dx}(e^y)=e^x+x^2$$ $$\Rightarrow e^y=e^x+\frac{x^3}{3}+C$$
Final: $\boxed{e^y=e^x+\frac{x^3}{3}+C}$
Factor Cue
Common Exponential: factor out $e^{-y}$ first.
Derivative Cue
Left-Side Upgrade: $e^y y'$ is $\dfrac{d}{dx}(e^y)$.
Finish Cue
Direct Integral: once linearized, integrate term by term.

Q13. $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=1+\tan(y-x)$

$$z=y-x,\qquad \frac{dz}{dx}=\frac{dy}{dx}-1$$ $$\Rightarrow \frac{dz}{dx}=\tan z$$ $$\Rightarrow \cot z\,dz=dx$$ $$\Rightarrow \ln|\sin z|=x+C$$ $$\Rightarrow \sin(y-x)=Ce^x$$
Final: $\boxed{\sin(y-x)=Ce^x}$
Sub Cue
Given Substitution: use $z=y-x$ immediately.
Trig Cue
Cot Route: $1/\tan z=\cot z$ gives the standard integral.
Finish Cue
Exponentiate: turn the log form into the sine form.
