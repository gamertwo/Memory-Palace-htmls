Exam View: Compact Solution

This is the stripped-down retrieval version. No theater, just the algebra in the right order.

Step 1: Reciprocal Swap

  • Let $$z=\dfrac{1}{y}.$$ Then $$\dfrac{dz}{dx}=-\dfrac{1}{y^2}\dfrac{dy}{dx},$$ so $$\dfrac{1}{y^2}\dfrac{dy}{dx}=-\dfrac{dz}{dx}.$$

Step 2: Rewrite as Linear

  • Substitute $$\dfrac{1}{y}=z$$ and $$\dfrac{1}{y^2}\dfrac{dy}{dx}=-z'.$$
  • Then $$-z'-z=2xe^{-x},$$ hence $$z'+z=-2xe^{-x}.$$

Step 3: Integrating Factor

  • Here $$P(x)=1,$$ so $$\mu(x)=e^{\int 1\,dx}=e^x.$$

Step 4: Product-Rule Collapse

  • Multiply by $$e^x$$: $$\dfrac{d}{dx}(ze^x)=-2x.$$
  • Integrate: $$ze^x=-x^2+C.$$

Step 5: Back-Substitute

  • Replace $$z=\dfrac{1}{y}:$$ $$\dfrac{e^x}{y}=-x^2+C.$$
  • Rearrange to get $$\boxed{e^x+x^2y+Cy=0}.$$

One-Line Retrieval Rule

Reciprocal pair -> use $$z=1/y$$ -> rewrite to linear -> fire the integrating factor $$e^x$$ -> collapse on the product mat -> case and coat for the final answer.
