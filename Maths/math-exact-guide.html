How To Study Exercise 3.7

The win condition here is not memorizing 10 unrelated answers. It is forcing the exact-equation method to feel automatic. Every page repeats the same five-step structure so the method, not the individual algebra, becomes the thing you remember under pressure.

$$M(x,y)\,dx+N(x,y)\,dy=0,\qquad M_y=N_x,\qquad F_x=M,\qquad F_y=N$$
Core Route

1. Standardize

Rewrite the equation into $$Mdx+Ndy=0$$ if the terms start on opposite sides.

2. Test

Check exactness with $$M_y$$ and $$N_x.$$ If they match, keep going.

3. Integrate

Integrate $$M$$ with respect to $$x$$ and add the missing $$g(y).$$

4. Repair

Differentiate your candidate with respect to $$y$$ and compare with $$N$$ to recover $$g'(y).$$

5. Finish

Substitute the missing correction back in and write the potential function equal to a constant.

What To Memorize
Trigger
If $$M_y=N_x,$$ think exact potential.
Main Unknown
The only missing piece after the x-integration is $$g(y).$$
Finishing Rule
Exact equations end as $$F(x,y)=C.$$
