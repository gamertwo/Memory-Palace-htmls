Recognition Rooms

Room 1: Homogeneous Signal

  • If dividing the equation reveals a strong $$y/x$$ pattern, move into the substitution zone $$y=vx.$$
  • Then use the product rule carefully: $$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=v+x\dfrac{dv}{dx}.$$
  • This is the recognition move used in Question 1.

Room 2: Quadratic In $$p$$

  • If the equation is algebraic in $$p,$$ check whether it factors before trying anything more advanced.
  • Each factor creates a branch equation after replacing $$p$$ with $$dy/dx.$$
  • This is the main move in Question 3.

Room 3: True Clairaut Form

  • If the equation already reads $$y=px+f(p),$$ it is a Clairaut equation.
  • The general solution comes from replacing $$p$$ by a constant.
  • This is the direct recognition step in Question 5.
Reusable Symbol Shapes
$$x$$
A horizontal rail or path. It means the direction we move along the problem.
$$y$$
A vertical tower or height-banner attached to the $$x$$ rail.
$$p=\dfrac{dy}{dx}$$
A slope spear that measures how sharply the tower changes along the rail.
$$p^2$$
A doubled slope spear inside a square shell, meaning the slope is being multiplied by itself.
Multiplication
Two objects fused together into one body, such as $$xp$$ or $$xy.$$
Division
A top object resting over a denominator platform, such as $$y/x$$ or $$dy/y.$$
Constants
Colored seals or crystals. They can be renamed from $$c$$ to $$a$$ without changing the solution family.
Substitution
One object folding into a new shape, like the $$y$$ tower folding into $$vx.$$
Differentiation
A sharp slash that creates motion pieces such as $$v+x\,dv/dx.$$
Integration
A long collecting arc or staircase that accumulates pieces into a new structure.
Brackets
A chamber wall that keeps grouped algebra traveling together before expansion.
Roots
A split hammer or branch gate that creates multiple sign options.
Inverse trig / inverse hyperbolic style
A curved return gate used when integration produces a named inverse function or logarithmic equivalent.
Connected Palace Route
Homogeneous Hall → Branch Forge → Clairaut Terrace

The route is continuous on purpose. Question 1 teaches substitution and separation, Question 3 teaches factoring into branches, and Question 5 finishes with direct pattern recognition. That makes the memory system feel like one long palace instead of separate disconnected cards.
