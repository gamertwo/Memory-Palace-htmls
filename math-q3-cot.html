Q3 · Cot Variant

$$\frac{dx}{x}=\cot y\,dy$$

Use $\int\cot y\,dy=\ln|\sin y|$.

$$\ln|x|=\ln|\sin y|+C\Rightarrow \ln\left|\frac{x}{\sin y}\right|=C$$
$$\boxed{\frac{x}{\sin y}=C}$$
