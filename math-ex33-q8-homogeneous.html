Exercise 3.3 Question 8

This is homogeneous. Use $y=vx$, reduce the equation to a logarithmic integral, and then clear the powers of $x$ in the final step.

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{x^2-2y^2}{2xy}$$
Rooms

Room 1: Put in Ratio Form

  • Write the right side in terms of $$\dfrac{y}{x}:$$
  • $$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{1-2\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)^2}{2\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)}.$$

Room 2: Use $y=vx$

  • Put $$y=vx,$$ so
  • $$\frac{dy}{dx}=v+x\frac{dv}{dx}.$$
  • Then
  • $$v+x\frac{dv}{dx}=\frac{1-2v^2}{2v}.$$

Room 3: Simplify

  • Subtract $v$ from both sides:
  • $$x\frac{dv}{dx}=\frac{1-4v^2}{2v}.$$

Room 4: Separate Variables

  • Bring the $v$ terms left:
  • $$\frac{2v}{1-4v^2}\,dv=\frac{dx}{x}.$$

Room 5: Integrate

  • Integrate both sides:
  • $$\int \frac{2v}{1-4v^2}\,dv=\int \frac{dx}{x}.$$
  • $$-\frac{1}{4}\ln|1-4v^2|=\ln|x|+C.$$
  • So
  • $$1-4v^2=\frac{C}{x^4}.$$

Room 6: Back-Substitute

  • Replace $$v=\frac{y}{x}:$$
  • $$1-\frac{4y^2}{x^2}=\frac{C}{x^4}.$$
  • Multiply by $$x^4:$$
  • $$x^4-4x^2y^2=C.$$
  • Rearranging gives
  • $$\boxed{4y^2-x^2=\frac{C}{x^2}}.$$
Final Answer

The solution reduces to $$\boxed{x^2(4y^2-x^2)=C},$$ which is usually written as $$\boxed{4y^2-x^2=\frac{C}{x^2}}.$$
