Q1 · Separable Trig-Log Question

Problem statement and full worked solution only for this question.

$$\frac{dx}{x}=\tan y\,dy$$
Step 1: Integrate both sides: $\int\frac{dx}{x}=\int\tan y\,dy$.
Step 2: Evaluate integrals: $\ln|x|=-\ln|\cos y|+C$.
Step 3: Merge logs: $\ln|x|+\ln|\cos y|=C\Rightarrow \ln|x\cos y|=C$.
Step 4: Exponentiate and absorb constant sign: $x\cos y=C$.
$$\boxed{x\cos y=C}$$
