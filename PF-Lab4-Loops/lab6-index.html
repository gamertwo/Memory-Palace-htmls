Programming Fundamentals Lab 6

Introduction to Recursive Functions

Lab 6 shifts from repeated loops to self-calling functions. The memory trick is simple: every recursive solution must know when to stop and how to shrink the problem before the next call.

$$\text{base case} + \text{smaller recursive case} \Rightarrow \text{correct recursion}$$
