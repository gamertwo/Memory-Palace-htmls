Core Math Pattern
Most of these problems reduce to one of a few moves: separate variables, detect homogeneity, simplify a derivative fingerprint, or normalize into a linear/separable form. The goal is the same each time: identify the trigger, choose the method, and preserve the final constant cleanly.
🧭 Method Flow
I · Scan
What trigger appears?
Look for already-separable form, equal degree terms, or derivative fingerprints like $\sec y\tan y$.
II · Choose
Pick the method
Use separation, the homogeneous substitution $y=vx$, or a direct integration pattern.
III · Integrate
Work both sides cleanly
Keep signs straight and use the correct antiderivative identity.
IV · Back-Sub
Restore original variables
If you used a substitution, return to $x$ and $y$ before finishing.
V · Seal
Clean the constant
Compress logs, exponentiate when needed, and rewrite the final relation in its neatest form.
