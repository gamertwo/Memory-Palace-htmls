Do and Do Not

Do

  • Choose the particle of interest before writing any force direction.
  • Put the force vector tail on the particle that feels the force.
  • Use magnitudes in Coulomb's law and let the diagram set the direction.
  • Resolve angled forces into components before adding them.
  • Use conservation before charge-sharing arithmetic.

Do Not

  • Do not let signs inside the formula replace the repel-attract logic.
  • Do not add magnitudes when the arrows point differently.
  • Do not search for equilibrium where both forces point the same way.
  • Do not forget the shell rule changes inside versus outside behavior.
  • Do not break total charge conservation in reactions or contact problems.
