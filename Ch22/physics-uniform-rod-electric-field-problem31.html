Problem 31: Field of a Uniformly Charged Rod

A rod of length $$L=8.15\text{ cm}$$ carries total charge $$-q=-4.23\text{ fC}$$ uniformly. Find charge density, the electric field at point $$P$$ located a distance $$a$$ from the rod end, the direction, and the far-field approximation comparison.

$$\lambda=-\frac{q}{L},\qquad E_x=-\frac{q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\,a(L+a)},\qquad E_x(a\gg L)\approx-\frac{q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 a^2}$$
Given Data
Rod Length
$$L=8.15\text{ cm}=0.0815\text{ m}$$
Total Charge
$$Q_{\text{rod}}=-4.23\text{ fC}=-4.23\times10^{-15}\text{ C}$$
Point Location
$$P$$ is at $$x=L+a$$ from left rod end
Part (b)
Use $$a=0.120\text{ m}$$
Part (d,e)
Use $$a=50\text{ m}$$
Constant
$$k=\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}=8.99\times10^9$$
Rooms: Full Solution (a) to (f)

Room 1: Part (a) Linear Charge Density

  • For uniform distribution,
  • $$\lambda=\frac{Q_{\text{rod}}}{L}=\frac{-4.23\times10^{-15}}{0.0815}=-5.19\times10^{-14}\text{ C/m}.$$

Room 2: Part (b) Set Up the Integral

  • Choose x-axis along the rod with origin at left end.
  • For element $$dx$$ at position $$x$$, charge is $$dq=\lambda\,dx.$$
  • Distance from element to point $$P$$ is $$r=(L+a)-x.$$
  • Its field contribution at $$P$$ is purely x-direction:
  • $$dE_x=\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{dq}{r^2}=\frac{\lambda}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{dx}{(L+a-x)^2}.$$

Room 3: Integrate from $$x=0$$ to $$x=L$$

  • $$E_x=\frac{\lambda}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\int_0^L\frac{dx}{(L+a-x)^2}.$$
  • The integral evaluates to
  • $$\int_0^L\frac{dx}{(L+a-x)^2}=\frac{1}{a}-\frac{1}{L+a}=\frac{L}{a(L+a)}.$$
  • So
  • $$E_x=\frac{\lambda L}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\,a(L+a)}=-\frac{q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\,a(L+a)}.$$

Room 4: Numerical Field for $$a=0.120\text{ m}$$

  • Use $$q=4.23\times10^{-15}\text{ C}$$ as magnitude and keep the minus sign in formula:
  • $$E_x=-\frac{(8.99\times10^9)(4.23\times10^{-15})}{(0.120)(0.0815+0.120)}\text{ N/C}.$$
  • $$\boxed{E_x=-1.57\times10^{-3}\text{ N/C}},\qquad |E_x|=1.57\times10^{-3}\text{ N/C}.$$

Room 5: Part (c) Direction

  • The negative sign means the field is along $$-x$$ direction.
  • So relative to +x axis, direction is $$180^\circ$$ (or $$-180^\circ$$).

Room 6: Part (d) Large-Distance Approximation

  • If $$a\gg L,$$ then $$L+a\approx a.$$
  • So the exact expression becomes
  • $$E_x\approx-\frac{q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 a^2}.$$
  • At $$a=50\text{ m},$$
  • $$\boxed{E_x\approx-1.52\times10^{-8}\text{ N/C}},\qquad |E_x|\approx1.52\times10^{-8}\text{ N/C}.$$

Room 7: Parts (e) and (f) Replace Rod by Point Charge

  • Replace rod by one particle of charge $$-q=-4.23\text{ fC}$$ at rod location.
  • Field at $$a=50\text{ m}$$ is exactly point-charge form:
  • $$E_x=-\frac{q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 a^2}=-1.52\times10^{-8}\text{ N/C}.$$
  • Magnitude matches part (d), confirming far-field behavior.
Key Physics Shortcut

Rod to Point-Charge Limit

Once you derive the exact rod field $$E_x=-q/[4\pi\varepsilon_0\,a(L+a)],$$ the far limit is immediate by replacing $$L+a$$ with $$a.$$ That gives the point-charge behavior at large distance.

Memory Palace Path

The route is strict: density first, geometry second, integration third, then signs and approximation.

Station 1: Density Desk

Compute $$\lambda=Q/L$$ and keep the negative sign.

Transition 1 to 2

After density, every tiny slice has charge $$dq=\lambda dx.$$

Station 2: Geometry Bridge

Distance to point $$P$$ is $$r=L+a-x.$$

Transition 2 to 3

With $$dq$$ and $$r$$ fixed, Coulomb differential is ready.

Station 3: Integral Hall

Integrate $$dE_x\propto dx/(L+a-x)^2$$ from $$0$$ to $$L.$$

Transition 3 to 4

The exact formula gives both magnitude and direction in one shot.

Station 4: Sign Vault

Negative result means field points toward the rod, along $$-x.$$

Transition 4 to 5

For very large distance, treat the rod as almost a point charge.

Station 5: Far-Field Door

Use $$L+a\approx a$$ to get $$E_x\approx-q/(4\pi\varepsilon_0 a^2).$$

Transition 5 to 6

This is exactly the same as replacing the rod by one charge $$-q.$$

Station 6: Match Seal

At $$a=50\text{ m},$$ both methods give $$|E_x|=1.52\times10^{-8}\text{ N/C}.$$
