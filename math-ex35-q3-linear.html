Exercise 3.5 Question 3

This is a linear equation with a trigonometric coefficient. The key observation is that the integrating factor for $$\cot x$$ is $$\sin x,$$ which turns the left side into the derivative of $$y\sin x.$$

$$\frac{dy}{dx}+y\cot x=\cos x\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad y\sin x=\frac{\sin^2 x}{2}+C$$
Quick Data
Method
First-order linear ODE
Standard Form
$$y'+(\cot x)y=\cos x$$
Here
$$P(x)=\cot x,\quad Q(x)=\cos x$$
Integrating Factor
$$\mu(x)=e^{\int \cot x\,dx}=\sin x$$
Textbook Form
$$y\sin x=\frac{\sin^2 x}{2}+C$$
Rooms: Full Solution

Room 1: Identify the Linear Form

  • The equation is already in the standard linear form
  • $$y'+P(x)y=Q(x).$$
  • Here
  • $$P(x)=\cot x,\qquad Q(x)=\cos x.$$

Room 2: Compute the Integrating Factor

  • Use
  • $$\mu(x)=e^{\int P(x)\,dx}.$$
  • So
  • $$\mu(x)=e^{\int \cot x\,dx}=e^{\ln|\sin x|}.$$
  • On a fixed interval, write
  • $$\mu(x)=\sin x.$$

Room 3: Multiply Through by $$\sin x$$

  • Multiply the whole equation by $$\sin x:$$
  • $$\sin x\frac{dy}{dx}+y\sin x\cot x=\sin x\cos x.$$
  • Since $$\sin x\cot x=\cos x,$$ this becomes
  • $$\sin x\frac{dy}{dx}+y\cos x=\sin x\cos x.$$
  • The left side is
  • $$\frac{d}{dx}(y\sin x).$$

Room 4: Integrate

  • So the equation reduces to
  • $$\frac{d}{dx}(y\sin x)=\sin x\cos x.$$
  • Integrate both sides:
  • $$y\sin x=\int \sin x\cos x\,dx.$$
  • Use $$u=\sin x,$$ so $$du=\cos x\,dx.$$
  • Then
  • $$y\sin x=\int u\,du=\frac{u^2}{2}+C=\frac{\sin^2 x}{2}+C.$$

Room 5: Final Answer

  • The textbook answer is
  • $$\boxed{y\sin x=\frac{\sin^2 x}{2}+C}.$$
  • Equivalently, $$y=\frac{\sin x}{2}+C\csc x$$ on intervals where $$\sin x\neq0.$$

Key Shortcut

Whenever the coefficient of $$y$$ is $$\cot x,$$ check whether multiplying by $$\sin x$$ will create a product derivative. That pattern appears constantly in trig-based linear equations.

Memory Palace Path

This route is linear-method plus one trig simplification: find the integrating factor, multiply, collapse to a product derivative, integrate once.

Station 1: Linear Gate

Tag the equation as $$y'+(\cot x)y=\cos x.$$

Transition 1 to 2

Station 2: IF Desk

Store $$\mu(x)=e^{\int\cot x\,dx}=\sin x.$$

Transition 2 to 3

Multiply by $$\sin x$$ so the trig term can simplify.

Station 3: Product Hall

Turn $$\sin x\,y'+y\cos x$$ into $$\dfrac{d}{dx}(y\sin x).$$

Transition 3 to 4

With the left side exact, only the trig integral remains.

Station 4: Integral Vault

Integrate $$\sin x\cos x$$ to get $$\sin^2 x/2+C.$$

Transition 4 to 5

The answer is already in the exact textbook form.

Station 5: Final Seal

Lock $$y\sin x=\frac{\sin^2 x}{2}+C.$$
